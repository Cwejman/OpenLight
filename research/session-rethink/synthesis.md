# Synthesis — what is a session

> **Provenance.** A fresh session (2026-07-07), deliberately without the rebirth epoch's context, asked to rethink the session from first principles with nothing taken as settled. Method: an own pass written down first (uncontaminated), then three parallel probes — [`standpoint.md`](standpoint.md) (blind derivation from the four mechanism specs only; the cleanroom recipe), [`critique.md`](critique.md) (adversarial UX attack, full context), [`forms.md`](forms.md) (generative pass over the citizenship space, full context). This file is the synthesis and the judgment calls. **Nothing here is folded into the specs — every proposal below awaits an author ruling.** The verdict up front: turns-only survives the rethink as the machine's half of the session; the person's half is missing one thing, and the *face* is pointed at the wrong ground.

---

## 1. One bug, found and verified

**agent.md contradicts session.md.** agent.md's pause section: *"While paused you inspect the trace, read what it read, even discuss the work in the conversation itself. Then `resume`."* Under session.md §1 there is nothing to discuss *with* — no message may land in an agent session (agent.md's own discourse section says messages belong to human conversations). Verified directly against both files. The round-thirteen reconciliation missed it. This must be resolved either way; which way is Proposal 1's question. The contradiction is diagnostic: the pause-and-discuss workflow the specs *want* requires a human voice in the session that turns-only deleted.

## 2. Convergence — four passes, one center

All four passes (own pass, blind, critique, forms) independently landed on the same three findings. By the project's own evidence standard, convergence of this strength is load-bearing.

**(a) The human voice below the model call.** Turns-only is machinery discipline misread as content law. The container's content is *deliberately wildcard* — the substrate already accepts a note placed on a session; only the gesture is missing. A person must be able to say something into the place where the work lives without paying for a completion: notes to self, thinking aloud, the re-entry breadcrumb, the pause-time discussion, the aside to a colleague. The blind pass derived this without ever seeing session.md (notes as ordinary content chunks, `instance` on the exchange with seq, `relates` on what they're about); the critique found the same wound from the opposite direction ("the input box is a loaded gun — a box you must not think into pushes thinking out of the field"; "the medium remembers everything except you").

**(b) The session conflates stance, trail, and face.** Three distinguishable things: the **stance** — where you stand (the standing selection, boundary template, toolset, guidelines; already field entities per session.md §7); the **trail** — the ordered turns, what happened while standing there; the **face** — what the session shows when encountered. The current spec identifies the session with the trail and derives the face from it (transcript of resting turns). The blind pass, given only the mechanisms, derived the *stance* as the primary entity — the standpoint: "completion from a point in the field" made literal, with the trail as accretion and the face as folds. Same chunk and placements either way; the difference is centering, and centering drives every UX default.

**(c) The face must not default to the raw trail.** The transcript is a strange attractor — renderers, habits, and third-party citizens calcify around it, and the deferred alternative never gets to be default. Three independent fixes, compatible and composable: the **mutation digest leads the resting fold** (the answer is commentary; what landed in the field is the product — the critique's sharpest point); the **standing brief** — one summary chunk over the session's turns, maintained by `narrate`/`summarize`, serving simultaneously as the session's list-name (auto-titling that cannot silently stale), the re-entry card, the fold face, the minimized widget, and the "include the session" projection for older turns (one chunk, five consumers, all mechanics specced); and **artifact-as-ground** for working sessions — the worked scope holds ground, live-updating under commits, with the dialogue docked as a rail (the forms pass's Workbench). In this system, watching an agent work is watching commits land in scopes; the transcript renders the frame, the field view renders the effects. Chat products can only do the first. The session experience should lead with the second — *you watch the work, not the log*.

## 3. Proposals — each needs a ruling

**P1 — Admit the note; settle one archetype.** One `conversation` type; three utterance kinds within it — **turn** (dispatch; the machinery's write), **note/message** (recorded, never completes), **control** (steering). What the input becomes is a property of the *gesture*, not the container: the dispatcher's Enter dispatches; a quiet gesture writes a note. "Agent session" vs "human conversation" differs only in which citizens are resident — the census, not the type. Context inclusion of notes is a dispatcher-policy knob riding "include the session" (lean: in by default — a deliberate note is the cheapest, highest-value context there is; pause-time meta-discussion stays out per the existing purity rule). This settles session.md §1's one-vs-two open, fixes the §1 bug above, and is the load-bearing repair: with it, everything else is policy and rendering. The blind pass adds the anchoring move: a correction is a note `relates`-placed *on the answer it corrects* — attached for every future reader in every context assembly, not later-in-one-list.

**P2 — Name stance/trail/face in session.md; the stance is of the session's essence.** Not a new archetype (the `conversation` type, answer-home rule, and dispatcher matching all stand) — a re-centering of §1: a session is a durable *point in the field* (its standing selection and defaults) with an accreting trail; the face is derived. Corollaries worth writing: forking is a new session `relates` on the old (no tree mechanics); a session never closes, it stops accreting; and **curation outward is the long arc's real motion** — settled products get placed onto their subject scopes, later turns read the field rather than the transcript, and a healthy session *thins*. The blind pass's formula: the transcript is the receipt; the field is the asset. Held open within P2, from the blind pass: whether the stance's relates-selection and the turn's read-boundary roots want to be the same chunk (`BoundarySpec`-shaped) — attractive unification, unproven semantics.

**P3 — Digest-first resting fold; Worklog-branchless as the v0.1 default face.** A completed work-turn rests as prompt + mutation digest + answer, digest always visible, answer folding when long; pure-Q&A turns (empty strip) rest prompt + answer as now — the form degrades to chat gracefully, chat never upgrades to it. This challenges §7's "reads as a conversation" resting rule head-on: that line should be marked *v0.1 staging*, not identity. Cost: a fold-line styling in the turn renderer plus a session header (cost + commit totals). No new citizen, no R1, no narrate.

**P4 — The standing brief, pulled forward.** The critique's sequencing finding: summary folds and `narrate` sit last in §6's implementation order, but they are load-bearing for use past week one (the lived v0.1 is otherwise an unfolded 80-turn scroll findable only by prompt-FTS). Either pull the brief earlier or accept that consciously. The brief composes onto the Worklog when it lands — same turns, one summary chunk over them.

**P5 — Write the destination ground into session.md.** A session with a related worked artifact grounds on the artifact (Workbench recipe: document ground, turn rail docked, dispatcher in the rail); transcript-ground is for sessions whose dialogue *is* the product. One sentence now, a view recipe later — but unwritten, the resting-form convention becomes the identity by default.

**P6 — Sessions are found from the field.** Conversations are entities related onto what they're about; the flat session list is the degenerate view (a scope query), not the primary navigation. "Needs you" is `sessions ∩ pending-obligations` — the forms pass's Tower (mission control over cards of briefs + live widgets) and Correspondence (inbox of gates/failures/unseen answers; emptiness is the success state; seen-markers via the existing `hidden` pattern) both fall out of scope queries plus existing mechanics. The Tower is also concrete evidence on the standing gate-placement open, for the *placed-onto-the-session* side.

**P7 — Typing while running is the correction.** The choreography tax on "actually, wait —": while a turn runs, the input row *is* the adjust channel — typing implies pause, Enter sends the distilled correction as `adjust`, resume follows. One gesture; the existing control taxonomy underneath; full pause-and-inspect stays as the deliberate form.

## 4. Where the probes were wrong — judgment calls

- **Standpoint as a replacement archetype** (blind pass): rejected as naming, adopted as centering (P2). The `conversation` type, answer-home rule, and dispatcher matching are settled and correct; the blind pass simply couldn't see them. What it proves is that the *mechanisms* point at the stance as the essence — exactly the recognition-evidence the cleanroom method is for.
- **"No message type at all"** (blind pass): overruled by mixed human conversations, which it explicitly couldn't derive (single-user files). The critique's utterance-kinds resolution (P1) is the landing; the blind pass's command/assert split is the same taxonomy from the other end.
- **Steering as plain notes** (blind pass — "no interrupt channel, just commit a note"): the existing `control` chunks are already this (chunks on the session), with the added value of being typed for the machinery. Keep controls; notes don't replace them.

## 5. Spaciousness census — what the mechanisms permit that no chat product can

Recorded so the building remembers the room it's in. Cross-session quoting: a turn frame dual-placed onto a second conversation — live, drillable, provenance intact; the conversation is a view over placements, not a place. Sessions nest: a conversation dropped into another renders at natural representation — its brief. Sessions as context: another session's scope added to the context list is cross-session memory without export. Alternation is policy: concurrent turns from one session interleave in the ordered scope; your-turn/my-turn isn't in the mechanism. Session-as-dashboard: table mode over turns × (cost, duration, status) via existing read-tile inference. Unread as a temporal query: diff against `at: <last-seen>` — no counters stored. Time-travel completion: re-run any turn against the session `at:` an old commit ("what would it have answered before we learned X"). Editable compaction: the summary the model stands on next turn is a person-editable chunk, not invisible auto-compaction. The prompt corpus: every verbatim request FTS-searchable, intersectable with commits. And the Takes (forms §6): one prompt fanned across models/contexts on branches, diffed by what each *wrote*, winner merged — the second strong pull on R1 after acceptance-merge.

## 6. Demands surfaced (for the ledger, not silence)

- **Slot scoped to a related scope** (Workbench): a view recipe must be able to give a slot the conversation *related on* the viewed artifact, not only the viewed scope itself. Within the slot-is-a-scope letter; needs saying.
- **Digest-first fold variant** as turn-renderer view-state (P3).
- **Gate placement**: fresh evidence for *also placed onto the session while pending* (Tower's waiting-strip; obligations are discourse-visible facts).
- **`note` in bootstrap** if P1 lands (archetype + accepts).
- No other new mechanism anywhere in the rethink — every form in [`forms.md`](forms.md) assembles from what the specs hold or hold open. The citizenship claim survives adversarial and generative pressure: the modularity is as spacious as suspected.
