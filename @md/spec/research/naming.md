# Naming — the search for what the system is called

*Research, 2026-08-23. Seeded from the framing exercise ([`framing.md`](framing.md)) at the author's direction: the name comes after the capability to frame, so every candidate answers to the ladder there. Nothing is ruled; **OpenLight** stands until a name earns succession. The author's read of the incumbent: it does not hold what the system really is.*

## What the name must do

- Survive the sentence rung: "*X* is a compute environment where …" — the name pulls weight, not rides along.
- Sit inside one of the tension pairings (substrate × surface · typed × lived · knowledge × compute · medium × environment · place × completion · field × citizen · commit × compound) rather than beside them.
- Leave room to grow: knowledge is the first tenant, not the last — a name bound to documents, notes, or AI ages badly.
- Practicals, checked late but before any adoption: collision search, domain, pronounceability across languages, how it shortens in speech (people will say something two-syllable regardless).

## Directions, by pairing

- **field / place** — Field, Openfield, Locus. Plain, spatial, matches "completion from a place". Field alone is likely uncollectable as a name; qualified forms get heavy.
- **ground / medium** — Loam, Ground, Bedrock. The compounding-substrate feel; loam is alive where bedrock is inert — aliveness is the right connotation for a medium things grow in.
- **joining** — Loom, Ply, Splice. Composition-forward. (*Weft* caught the author's eye elsewhere first — [`weft.md`](weft.md), another project in an adjacent space, not a peer of this one; the word is not forbidden, but the collision is real and documented.)
- **growth / compounding** — Coppice, the mycelium family. Organic compounding; trend-scent risk.
- **light** (the incumbent) — OpenLight: openness and clarity, carries the project's history; says nothing of field, type, or compute.

## Round one — breadth (2026-08-23, steward sweep)

Nine meaning-fields swept for candidates; each survivor tested in the sentence rung and collision-checked. Gut decides; this is the breadth to feel through.

**Tier 1 — strong matches.**

- **Topos** *(Greek: place; rhetoric: the commonplaces knowledge is drawn from; mathematics: a typed universe rich enough to reason inside)*. The triple meaning is almost unfair: *completion from a place*, knowledge's own storehouse, and a typed world — all in one word. Sentence rung: "Topos is a compute environment where …" — the name does half the sentence's work. Risks: academic scent; topos theory is a live math field (a flattering, non-product collision).

- **Mylla** *(Swedish: living topsoil — and the verb: to work something into the soil)*. The living ground where what falls compounds into fertility, and the act of folding-in — both estates of the compounding story in a homely word. Nordic identity travels well (the Spotify/Klarna precedent); ownable, pronounceable abroad. Sentence rung: carries ground and growth; compute rides the sentence, not the name.

- **Locus** *(Latin: place; mathematics: the set of points satisfying an expression; genetics: an addressable position)*. A locus is literally what a selection is — the place an expression describes. Clean, short, serious. Collisions: Locus Magazine, Locus Robotics — distant fields.

- **Glänta / Glenta** *(Swedish: a clearing in the forest; the verb: to open a door ajar)*. The lit, open place inside dense wood — continuity with OpenLight's light, plus place, plus the gentlest open. Collision: *Glänta*, a Swedish literary journal — same cultural sphere, worth weighing.

**Tier 2 — real, with a catch.**

- **Loam** — alive medium, short, warm; says ground, not compute.
- **Manifold** — *many folds*; a space locally simple everywhere — the knowledge thesis in one mathematical word. Collisions: the car part, Manifold Markets.
- **Lattice** — the hidden aptness: union, intersection, subtraction *is* lattice algebra — the boundary language's own mathematics. Collision: Lattice (HR software).
- **Plenum** — the fully-filled space *and* the assembly that governs; field + governance in one. Institutional scent.
- **Källa** *(Swedish: a spring — and a cited source)*. Provenance and living origin in one word; the umlaut is a real barrier abroad.

**Benchmarks, dead on collision** — named so the meaning is kept even where the word is lost: **Confluence** (flows joining into one — exactly the meaning wanted; Atlassian owns it in this very space) · **Foundry** (where type is cast — the *typed* pun; Palantir owns it) · **Commons** (the governed shared ground; Creative/Wikimedia own it) · **Weft** ([`weft.md`](weft.md)).

**Fields swept with nothing surviving**: water beyond källa/confluence (delta, ström), mythic names (Mnemosyne-class — too heavy), invented blends (all read as startups), civic beyond plenum/commons (agora — taken; forum, torg — market scent), type-craft beyond foundry (ligature — the medical sense; sort, matrix — taken or confusing).

**Steward's read, gut withheld**: Topos and Mylla pull hardest from opposite poles — Topos from the claim (place, type, knowledge), Mylla from the value (aliveness, compounding, folding-in). Locus is the safe strong middle. Glänta is the one that keeps OpenLight's soul.

## Round two — the fleet (2026-08-23, nine territories, ~430 candidates)

Nine parallel generators (Latin/Greek · Nordic · plain English · math/science · coined · world/civic/music · enablement · deep nature · history/culture), briefed with the ladder and the author's steers — name the *enablement*; mine nature, history and culture; hunt double meanings. Raw material and the full walkable longlist (561 names, triaged) retired to git with the tidy of 2026-08-23 — their yield is folded below; this file is the arc's single home. Collision/SEO pass run on the standouts via web search.

**Convergence signal** (independent territories finding the same word): Cambium ×3, Chora/Khora ×3, Koine ×2, Cairn ×2, Continuo ×2, Tilth ×2.

**The standing set** — meaning strong *and* name-space open:

- **Tilth** — soil worked into the condition where things take root: the quality of a medium measured by what grows in it. One quiet syllable, night-compatible. *Software-clean.*
- **Continuo** — the ground line that keeps sounding while every voice builds freely above it; the musical true-name of "the field beneath everything." *Exact name free; the sound-neighborhood is crowded (Continu, Continia, Continuum).*
- **Grogrund** — Swedish, literally "sprout-ground": seedbed. Idiomatic in the author's own tongue — *en grogrund för* = "a breeding ground for (innovation, ideas)"; the name *is* the enablement sentence in one word. *Software-clean; SLU Grogrund (Swedish plant-breeding programme) exists outside software.*
- **Meristem** — the plant tissue where all growth originates, each cell typed by its position: growth at an addressed edge. *Near-clean in software.*
- **Heddle** — the loom part that decides, pass by pass, which threads lift: the piece that *types* the weave. Obscure enough to own outright. *Clean.*
- **Selvedge** — the edge where cloth binds itself and cannot unravel: contracts holding at every boundary. *Clean in software; premium-denim connotation.*
- **Vang** — Old Norse open meadow (Folkvang, the field of the people, behind it). Short, sayable anywhere. *Clean-ish.*
- **Varve** — the sediment layer that makes each year separately readable forever: a typed, versioned field as a geological fact. *One small IT firm.*
- **Craton** — the ancient stable core everything since has accreted onto. *Small firms only.*
- **Velm** — pure coinage, no argument, only the right mouth: velvet/realm/loam at night. *Unchecked, likely clean.*

**Meaning-benchmarks lost to collision** — kept because the *meaning* should steer even where the word cannot: **Pando** (one root system, tens of thousands of stems — the mycelium thought in its purest form; funded logistics startup) · **Cambium** (the living layer between substrate and surface where all growth happens, root meaning *exchange*; two sizable companies) · **Chora/Khora** (Plato's receptacle — that-in-which things come to be; crowded, incl. a commons-governance platform) · **Koine** (the common tongue that replaced walled dialects; defunct crypto + firms) · **Tessera** (the trust-token and the mosaic tile; a16z-funded AI startup) · **Cairn** (every traveler adds a stone and the pile is the way; several small software firms — *weighable*, not clean) · **Sangam** (rivers meeting as an academy; Indian IT firms) · **Otta** (the hour before dawn — the mood-perfect night-to-light word; the jobs platform, now Welcome to the Jungle) · **Tila** (Finnish: place, room *and* state; tila.ai) · **Sheaf** (the local-to-global gluing mechanism; unchecked) · **Sampo** (the mill that grinds abundance; the insurer).

**Steward's tiering, gut withheld** — by meaning × ownability: **Tilth, Grogrund, Continuo** front rank (ground-alive · the author's own tongue and the enablement literal · the music of composition); **Meristem, Heddle** for the mechanism-lovers; **Cairn** if a small-collision fight is acceptable for the best enablement image; **Velm/Vang** if the name should carry mood and let the sentence carry meaning.

## The author's walk (2026-08-23) — 78 keepers, and the taste made legible

- **Enablement doubles** — Yield ×2 · Seam · Braid ×2 · Moot · Endow · Cay · Motu · Alder ×2 · Reef ×3 · Serai · Hearth · Stalk · Fiber.
- **Deep-meaning polysyllables kept despite the style** — Holon · Monad · Cosmos · Operad · Closure · Gamma · Biotope ×2 · Mycel · Noctua · Geode.
- **Mythic thread** — Embla · Ymir · Saga · Terma · Kete (partnership-gated) · Ting ×2 · Odal (rune-appropriation flag).
- **Mood coinage / mood words** — Velm · Embra · Rime · Haar · Imum · Forma · Mitos · Typos · Avara · Jumla · Aula.

**The inferred taste profile** (for any future round): one or two syllables; concrete over abstract; Anglo-Saxon/Norse over Latin; dark or soft vowels, closed endings (Velm, Holm, Tarn, Wold); words that name a *working thing* (kiln, quern, tiller) or a *joining act* (round, catch, seam); quiet, unbranded, night-compatible; real double meanings prized; cliché-Latin and startup-scented coinage rejected even when apt.

## The medium round (2026-08-23, closing the session)

The author ruled the category: **a computing medium** — knowledge is one thing you do with it (a field synthesizer is another); medium holds across substrate, application and interface. So the name should name *a medium*: a substance things enter, live in, and compound in. Steward-generated directly under that light:

- **Gesso** — the prepared ground a painter lays before anything is painted: painting's own word for "the ground everything else is made on." Craft-warm, two syllables, obscure enough to own.
- **Grain** — the structure inherent in a medium (wood grain, film grain, against the grain) — and the spec's *own internal word* (the grain rule). One syllable, concrete. [Grain, the meeting-recorder product, is a real collision.]
- **Leaven** — the living agent that, worked through a medium, makes the whole of it rise; "to leaven the lump" is the enablement as idiom. Quiet, deep.
- **Ferment** — the working aliveness in a medium, and "a ferment of ideas" — intellectual excitement as the word's own second sense.
- **Amber** — the medium that preserves whatever enters it, visible forever: losslessness as a substance; night-golden. [Crowded-ish: alerts, crypto.]
- **Vellum** — the durable writing medium, made to be written on and to last. [Vellum book-software exists.]
- **Marrow** — the living medium inside the standing structure, where the blood is made; "to the marrow."
- **Patch** — the modular-synth word for a composition of modules wired live (the author's own music world) *and* the code word. [Generic + Patch media.]
- **Lera** — Swedish: clay, the shapeable medium; the aurr/Völuspá clay one language nearer home.
- **Virke** — Swedish: timber as working material — the stuff you build from. [Virke is Norway's enterprise federation.]
- **Stoff** — Swedish/German: fabric, matter — and the *material of a story* (stoff till en berättelse). The medium double in the author's own tongue.
- **Amne / Ämne** — Swedish: both *subject* (skolämne) and *raw material* (råämne) — topic and matter in one word; the umlaut is the price.
- **Moss** · **Felt** · **Culture** · **Starter** — softer seconds: the spreading cover, the fiber-compounded cloth (felt = experienced; the Felt mapping startup collides), the living culture (unownable word), the sourdough starter passed hand to hand, fed by each keeper. Named for the vein even where the word fails.
