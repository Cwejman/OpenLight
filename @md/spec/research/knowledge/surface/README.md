# The surface

Knowledge under the code stands in a shape, and nothing it is written in lets a reader move by that shape. A document makes you scroll past whatever you meant to skip. A directory listing gives you names and nothing of the shape they stand in.

The surface is the interface those two are missing. It reads a body of knowledge by its own shape (its topology), standing on the markdown and the directories rather than replacing them. A session's own work is read the same way once the session has written it down.

## 1. What is refused, and what that buys

This builds no new substrate and no new harness. The medium [the code's sixth section](../code.md#6-how-far-this-goes) reaches for is not this and is not built from this. The substrate stays prose in markdown and git. The harness stays the one that exists, its context appended rather than composed.

Refusing both is what makes the rest cheap, because one interface then serves two moments of the same repository. Locally it reads a working tree, again as the files change, which is the live view a session needs. Published, it reads what a build emits from that same tree, so a repository's site is the repository rather than a site kept in step by hand.

It also supplies what the harness does not. A harness shows very little of what a session is doing, and keeps what it does show in a transcript outside the repository the work produced. A session that writes its own work into the substrate is read like everything else.

So the surface needs no knowledge of harnesses. It is handed a path in a working tree, never reads above it, and digests only what [the stamp](../poc.md#9-a-file-says-it-is-under-the-code) declares to be knowledge, so a directory is onboarded a file at a time and whatever wants to be read writes itself into the substrate first.

*Reasoned, the author's, 2026-09-12.*

## 2. [A level to a pane](panes.md)

Wherever you are reading you are at a path in the holarchy, and no brief is orphaned, since each is a part of the understanding above it. That is what a surface can be built on: there is always somewhere you are, and always a way you came.

So the reading is laid out the way a path is. Each level of the path takes a pane, the panes stand side by side, and going deeper adds one on the right without taking any away, so where a reader came from stays present while they read.

*Reasoned, the author's, 2026-09-12.*

## 3. [What the prose cannot say](drawings.md)

Prose says what a brief means and cannot say what it is: how large, what rhythm its paragraphs have, where it sits, how much hangs beneath it. Those are facts of shape, and shape is seen rather than read.

So the surface draws them, and the code says why it must. A reader judges what a part will cost from its look before spending any reading on it, and that is the judgment [the labs failed most often](../study/findings.md#1-heaviness-is-seen-before-it-is-read), seen there and carried here unchecked. A rule never fixed it and a picture did.

*Reasoned, the author's, 2026-09-12.*

## 4. [Moving, and asking](moving.md)

A reader does three things besides read. They go deeper, they go sideways, and they ask about a brief without going anywhere at all. The first two change the path and are remembered. The third leaves no trace.

That division is what makes looking free. A reader can see what a descent would give before spending it, and nothing they merely looked at follows them.

*Reasoned, the author's, 2026-09-12.*

## 5. What is open

Whether the harness can be modified after all. Its SDK could assemble a context from the substrate rather than append to a trail, which is the largest gain in reach here. Unanswered.

What a session keeps. The panes and the drawing read any substrate, so they read a session's record once it is written down, but nothing here says what a session should write, or how it is made to write it while it works rather than at its close.

*Open, 2026-09-12. What each brief leaves open stands at its own foot; these two belong to no one of them.*

## 6. What is not yet taken up

The technical ground is not covered here and is not owed by these briefs. How the markdown is parsed into the structure the practice defines, in what unit a weight is counted, what happens as files change, what a reader without a pointer is given, what a published build emits and how it is hosted: none of it is written, and none of it is decided by what is.

That is deliberate. These briefs say what the surface is to be, and the ground beneath them is settled next, against them.

*In force as a boundary on this holon, 2026-09-12.*

## 7. [What was tried and refused](refused.md)

A reader and its nine briefs were built in one sitting on 2026-09-11, each change made before the last had been used, and both were retired to git the next day. These briefs are the specification written first instead, and no program has yet been written against them.

What that first cut reached for is kept beneath, each shape with the reason it is not wanted, so that none of them is put forward again as though it were new. Nothing there directs the build except by exclusion.

*Seen, in the first cut and in the author's reading of it; each refusal beneath names the rule or the observation that grades it. It is also the reason [the study names running ahead](../study/findings.md#7-building-runs-ahead-more-easily-than-writing-does) as a finding of its own.*
