# The surface

Knowledge under the code stands in a shape, and neither of the two things it is written in lets a reader move by that shape. A document makes you scroll past whatever you meant to skip. A directory listing gives you names and nothing of the shape they stand in.

The surface is the interface those two are missing. It reads a body of knowledge by its own shape (its topology), standing on the markdown and the directories rather than replacing them. A session's own work is read the same way.

## 1. What the compromise buys

This builds no new substrate and no new harness, and it is not the typed medium [the code's sixth section](../code.md#6-how-far-this-goes) reaches for. The substrate stays prose in markdown and git. The harness stays the one that exists, its context appended rather than composed.

Refusing both is what makes the rest cheap, because one interface then serves two moments of the same repository. Locally it reads a working tree, again as the files change, which is the live view a session needs. Published, it reads what that same tree emits, so a repository's site is the repository rather than a site kept in step by hand.

It also supplies what the harness does not. A harness shows very little of what a session is doing, and keeps what it does show in a transcript outside the repository the work produced. A session that writes its own work into the substrate is read like everything else, live while it runs and afterwards.

None of that joins the surface to the harness. It reaches for no log and knows nothing of harnesses. Whatever wants to be read becomes substrate first, and [what a session writes]() is owed beyond [the skill](../skill.md).

*Decided, the author's, 2026-09-12.*

## 2. What is not decided

Whether the harness can be modified after all. Its SDK could assemble a context from the substrate rather than append to a trail, which is the largest gain in reach here, and whether it is affordable turns on whether the SDK bills against a subscription or per use. Unanswered, and cheap to answer.

And what the surface actually does. How a body is read, how a reader is shown what stopping costs, what a session keeps: none of it is settled, and this entry orders those briefs when they exist.

A reader and its nine briefs were built in one sitting on 2026-09-11 and retired to git the next day, so the understanding stands before the program does. That is why there is nothing beneath this entry yet.
