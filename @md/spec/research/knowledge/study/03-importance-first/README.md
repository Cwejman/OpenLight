# Lab 03 — importance first, and nothing else

The first of three labs that took the narrative away and tested structure on its own. This one gave the writer a single rule: the biggest understanding comes first, and each thing after it stands on what came before.

Nesting was not asked for and the spine rules were not given. What the lab isolates is whether ordering by importance, by itself, carries a reader who has no story to follow.

[The piece](output.md) is the output, and [the prompt](prompt.md) is what produced it.
