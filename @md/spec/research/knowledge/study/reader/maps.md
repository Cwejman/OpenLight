# How it maps

These are the views of the whole that stand beside [the reading](reads.md), each the same substrate at a different distance. What they mark and how they answer the pointer is [how it marks](marks.md); the grades of figure still owed are in [the path forward](forward.md#3-the-figures).

The column beside the spreads holds the views of the whole. Each is the same substrate at a different distance, and each is a section you can close, remembered between visits, so the estate is the reader's to spend.

## 1. Where you are, from the root

One row per step of the descent, each carrying the name of the room you passed through, what it holds in its own prose and what waits beneath it, and the siblings you did not take as weighted marks with the one you did lit. Rooms you have been in before are marked among those siblings, so the descent and your own path read in one figure.

## 2. The figure

A radial of the room you stand in: each arc a room, its angle the text beneath it, its hue the region it belongs to, its shade the depth. An arc says its name whole, or only its weight, or nothing; a cut-off word is worse than silence. The hub carries what is being read here against what waits in the rooms, that brief's own paragraphs as ticks, a ring that fills as the prose is read, and a needle that swings to whatever the pointer is on. Standing on a leaf, the figure roots at the room you are in rather than vanishing.

Links are drawn as chords across it, so the crossings of the holarchy are visible rather than only followable.

## 3. The whole, divided as it divides

The space divides as the holarchy divides: each room a region of its parent weighted by what it holds, its own prose a darker band inside it, labelled where it is large enough. Division is what a holarchy is, so the picture and the thing agree, and a cell is something a pointer can find. It divides only while a room can still be seen; below that floor it stops, marks itself as holding more, and hands that depth to the pointer.

## 4. The gauge

What stopping at each level costs: the prose owned at each depth beneath where you stand, and the running total. It is the code's promise made checkable. If the biggest understanding really does come first, the top is short and each level below it heavier.

## 5. The files, as they lie

The filesystem alone, indented, opening and closing, each file carrying its region's hue and its weight. The abstract division and the explicit tree stand beside each other rather than one instead of the other.

## 6. The page you are on

A strip lying along the page, position running left to right and the band's height saying what each part is, with the viewport a window along it. Click anywhere on it to go there.
