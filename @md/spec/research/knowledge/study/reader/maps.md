# How it maps

A reader who can see only the room they are in is reading a file again. So beside [the spreads](reads.md) stands a column of figures, each drawing the whole at a different distance: the path you took, the shape of what is beneath you, the weight of it, and the page under your eye. Each is a section you can close, and what is closed is remembered, so the room they take is yours to spend.

*Seen: these are the figures that exist. That there are too many of them for the space, and that each asks for room the reading wants, is the second fault named in [the path forward](forward.md#1-use-before-anything-more).*

## 1. Where you are, from the root

One row per step of the descent, each carrying the name of the room you passed through, what it holds in its own prose against what waits beneath it, and the rooms you did not enter as weighted marks with the one you did lit. Rooms you have been in before are marked among them, so the way down and the way you came read in one figure. *Seen.*

## 2. The radial

A radial of the room you stand in: each arc a room, its angle the text beneath it, its hue the region it belongs to, its shade the depth. An arc says its name whole, or only its weight, or nothing, because a cut-off word is worse than silence.

The hub carries four things and each is small: what is being read here against what waits in the rooms; that room's own paragraphs as ticks; a ring that fills as the prose is read; and a needle that swings to whatever the pointer is on. Standing on a leaf the figure roots at the room you are in rather than vanishing.

Links are drawn as chords across it, so that a room's relations show as well as its place, though a chord says only that a relation exists and not what it is. *Seen; and short of what was asked, which wanted the arcs to carry their own prose and the relations drawn properly. See [the path forward](forward.md#3-the-figures).*

## 3. The whole, divided as it divides

The space divides as the holarchy divides: each room a region of its parent weighted by what it holds, its own prose a darker band inside it, labelled where it is large enough. Division is what a holarchy is, so the picture and the thing agree, and a region is something a pointer can find where a line is not.

It divides only while a room can still be seen. Below that floor it stops and marks itself as holding more, and the room it could not draw is reached by pointing instead, which [how it marks](marks.md#1-the-hover-and-the-card) describes. *Reasoned: below the floor a region is too small to see, so the only honest thing left to do with that depth is hand it to the pointer.*

## 4. The gauge

What stopping at each level costs: the prose owned at each depth beneath where you stand, and the running total. It is the code's promise made checkable, since if the biggest understanding really does come first, the top is short and each level below it heavier. *Seen, and the one figure whose reading has been checked: on the piece this was built against it shows the promise failing, since that piece stops being short at its second level, which is what its own record says of it.*

## 5. The files, as they lie

The filesystem alone, indented, opening and closing, each file carrying its region's hue and its weight. The abstract division and the explicit tree stand beside each other rather than one instead of the other, because a reader who knows a repository wants to see it as it is. *Preferred; it was asked for after the division alone proved not to give it.*

## 6. The page you are on

A strip lying along the page, position running left to right and the band's height saying what each part is, with the viewport a window along it. Click anywhere on it to go there. *Seen.*
