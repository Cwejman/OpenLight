# How it reads

Depth runs to the right. Each level of a descent is a spread of its own, so entering a room does not replace the room you were in: it takes the column beside it, and the way you came stays legible as a row. Up to three spreads divide the width, and the rail above them carries the whole descent from the root, so going deeper than three drops a spread from view and never the knowledge of where it sits.

## 1. A spread presents doors

A spread shows one room: its name, what it weighs, its own paragraphs as a texture, its division, and its prose. Beneath that, its rooms as doors, each a name and a weight and a line of what it gives. Nothing beyond the room's own prose is loaded into a spread. A brief with twenty rooms is twenty ways on, not a wall to scroll.

## 2. The room ahead

A chosen room becomes a spread. The rooms not yet chosen all contend for the same next column, and that asks for its own mechanic rather than a click into the dark. That column belongs to whichever room the pointer rests on, wherever the pointer is: a door in the list, a segment of a division, an arc of the figure, a step of the descent. The room opens there as the spread it would become, marked as ahead. A click commits it.

So descent is committal and remembered, and looking ahead is free and leaves no trace: hovering never enters the trail and never marks a brief read.

## 3. One scroll

The spreads are one reading, not three. A scroll anywhere moves them all by the same distance, and a shorter spread reaches its end and waits, so the descent stays aligned while each holds what it holds. The head of every spread holds at its top while its prose runs beneath it.

## 4. Moving

Everything that names a room is a way to it: a door, a room in a list, a step in the rail, a name in the descent, a cell, an arc, a segment, a link in the prose, a name under what points here. Navigation goes through the browser's own history, so back and forward work and every room has an address that can be shared.

Links in the prose resolve to their targets and are followed inside the reader; one that leaves the substrate says so by its underline. Every brief also says what points at it, and a room the entry brings in is named as introduced rather than listed as though a stranger pointed at it.
