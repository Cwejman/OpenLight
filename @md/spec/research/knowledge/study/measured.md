**The gradient holds wherever it was written to.** Every body rises level over level, which is what the code promises: a reader who stops at the top reads a fraction of what waits beneath. The study whole runs 154 tokens at its entry against 145,899 five levels down, and the reader 249 against 5,291. Two exceptions, both explicable: the day's asks are a record and have no gradient to keep, and giving as a business falls at its third level because only one of its positions was broken into rooms.

**Heaviness is the standing fault.** 981 paragraphs across the study run past five hundred characters, the worst at 6,665, in a study whose own tools for drawing that were built by a session that wrote twelve such paragraphs the same night. The threshold is the practice's open question and not a settled measure; what is measured here is only the count.

**The night's own briefs are the lightest thing in the tree**, five heavy paragraphs in the reader's thirty-six and none at all in the one written last. That is what three cold reads and three folds do, and it is the only evidence in this record that the cycle works.

*Measured, by walking the tree with the reader's own parser. It counts prose and cannot judge it.*
