# Tenants beyond prose

Knowledge is the substrate's first tenant, and the code's sixth section says the shape reaches further. This file holds the cases as they occur, between the study and the project's vision: each a domain named as it would look as entries in the substrate, held open and marked so. Begun 2026-09-11.

## 1. Sound

A digital audio workstation as the substrate would hold it. A patch, an instrument's configuration, is a brief: whole and readable alone, and a part of a track, which is a part of an arrangement, which is a part of a piece. The temporal buffers, MIDI and audio, are the raw beneath, one link away from the patch that plays them, never restated inside it. The gradient runs from the piece to the note: a listener or a collaborator enters at the piece and descends as far as their need takes them, and stops whole at any level. Every edit is a step, the holon viewed by its change, so a session at the workstation leaves a record as a writing session does, and a track can be branched on its own without the whole piece carrying the conflict. Entries are hospitable here as anywhere: by instrument, by section, by the last change, by the session that made it. What the code asks of prose, a face that says what it gives and confidence written beside a claim, has its sound-shaped forms to be found: a patch that says what it sounds like before it is opened, a take marked as kept or superseded.

Reasoned, the author's, from a thought in passing; nothing built, and the first tenant after knowledge only because it was the one that came to mind.

## 2. Image

The visual holds the same way, and it brings the other half of the interface with it. A node-based environment such as TouchDesigner already works as a holarchy of operators: each node whole, readable alone, and a part of a network, with the signal flowing through as the buffers flow under a patch. Its artfulness is the point: composition by wiring, seen live. As entries in the substrate, a node is a brief and a network a holon, every parameter change a step, and the visualisation modules become the drawing components the practice's visuals section hypothesises, composed from what the text says rather than laid by hand. That is where the associative interface comes from: prose and the visual in one medium, a brief beside the shape it describes, the shape beside the sound it plays, and an entry to any of them from any other, so a reader moves by association rather than by a file tree.

Reasoned, the author's, the same night as sound; nothing built.
