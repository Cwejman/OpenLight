## What the cost says

The cost is mostly the writer's context, not the generation and not the blank heads. The context, the raw and the code, is re-sent on every message and read from cache at a tenth of the price, which over 38 messages is 1.27M. And it was written to the cache twice at full size: 113k at the start, and 446k at minute 34, after the five-minute cache had expired during round 3's silence. The writer's cache was the five-minute kind throughout; a one-hour cache would have spared the second write, about a sixth of the run.

The rounds themselves are cheap next to that. Taking the context out, the cycle roughly doubled the price of the draft, and the two blank heads together added a tenth.
