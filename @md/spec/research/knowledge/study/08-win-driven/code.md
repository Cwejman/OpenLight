# The code

This code turns written knowledge into a medium, one that joins us to what we know and to each other. Keeping it in order stops being a job of its own. You take part in it, and you add to it. This document is the whole code, and reading it leaves you able to write under it.

## 1. Why there is a code

This is the essence the rest of the code rests on: what knowledge is (its ontology), and what shape it takes (its topology). It leaves a good deal to your imagination, this is broken down further as the document progresses.

Comprehension is what most value is built on. Insight compounds when separate understandings join into a larger one. Collaboration rests on that same joining, across people.

That joining comes with friction. We meet it in our own progress, more in a team working toward a common goal, and most in large organisations. What makes it hard is having to join what each of us carries, across our different roles, background and processes.

Every gain in comprehension is a win. The biggest win is the one that gives the most understanding for the least reading.

The code is a set of principles for structuring knowledge into brief wins. The biggest wins come first, and each breaks down into smaller wins, down to every detail that grounds them.

## 2. What it changes

This is what the code is for first: what changes in practice once knowledge is written this way, and what it takes to begin.

People and models hit the same wall. A person forgets. A model's context fills and gets worse as it fills. Both must hold what matters and reach for the rest. So writing for people and building context for models are one problem, not two.

What changes first is who has to explain. Nobody has to be walked through it, and the person who knows a thing stops being the only way to it. A new colleague, or a fresh agent session, opens the top and reads down to the depth their task needs. The same decision is not made twice, because it has one home.

And keeping it in order stops being a job of its own. Each addition lands where it belongs, and the top stays short, so the knowledge gets better as it grows instead of worse. That follows from the shape of the knowledge itself, not from anyone's discipline. Most of the effort that remains is work AI compute can do. How much human judgment it still needs is not known, and review will almost certainly stay with people. You take part in the knowledge and add to it, and clarity, connection and faster shared comprehension are what come out.

Taken together, this is a substantial change in how we work.

None of this waits on new technology to propel its capability. Nothing here is typed. No schemas, no edge types, only prose in structured pieces under a defined law, which is why markdown in directories is enough. A [proof of concept]() runs this with git and the harnesses that exist today. It pays soonest in IT, where part of the group already works with them.

## 3. How far this goes

This part is for engineers, and the rest of the code does not depend on it. It asks how far the same shape reaches, and what would have to be built to take it there.

The code is written for prose, and much of it is about prose. The shape underneath is not: wholes that are also parts, arranged topologically so the biggest win comes first. Knowledge is the first case rather than the easiest one, because comprehension comes before building, and the rest still has to be built.

That shape holds beyond prose, but only on one condition: everything has to live in a unified medium, the way knowledge does here. Then a program can say what it takes and what it gives as structure in that same medium, so programs meet by matching instead of by custom glue. What draws a screen says the same. People, models and other programs are all citizens of that medium, and types are for the last of the three, which cannot read a sentence. A model becomes one of these programs, its context a selection from the medium and its output landing back as structure. The harness no longer exists, and all capabilities needed for computers to become the fully fledged medium land on simple grounds.

None of that exists today. You can assemble a context however you like, and branching and rollback ship, but what you assemble is made for the occasion and belongs to one tool, and the current agent harnesses are closed functions of a linear nature. Nobody can say an answer came from exactly this state and re-derive it. There is no shared medium to be a citizen of. A monolith is a bounded world, and it does have seams. They are for translation, so what compounds is the glue and never the parts. The code needs no new technology. This does, and it is not built. Beyond knowledge, all of this is reasoned and not shown. But first we change how we work with knowledge, using what already exists.

## 4. What knowledge is made of

This is the ontology [§1](#1-why-there-is-a-code) promised: the kinds of thing knowledge is made of once it is written under the code, and the one who comes to it. An ontology is the list of things you have to say exist before you can say anything else about a subject [Quine 1948]. Here the list is six, graded: each kind stands on the one before it, and each is a bigger win than the next, so a reader who stops early still holds the most that could be held. Nothing in the six names a technology.

### 4.1 The holon

A holon is a whole that is also a part [Koestler 1967]. It is the one kind of thing knowledge is made of here, at every scale: a section, a document, a whole field. Every holon has a surface and a depth. The surface is what it gives at once, its essence. The depth is what it keeps within, for whoever needs it, and the depth is itself made of holons.

In prose a holon is called a brief. The name means what everyday speech means by it. To brief someone is to hand them, quickly, the understanding they need to act. When a stretch of work ends it is debriefed: the conclusion goes to the surface, and the detail stays within. A brief is brief because the reader came to be briefed, not to relive the work.

A holon holds one thing. When it grows to hold two, it is broken down into holons within it or beside it, until each stands on its own. Nothing is lost in the breaking. The detail moves a level deeper, not away.

### 4.2 Two relations

Holons join in two ways, and only two. Within: one holon is part of another, and this is the nesting the depth is made of. Upon: one holon stands on another, and this is what a reader must already hold for the first to be understood. Written down, upon is a link. Followed all the way, upon is ground: everything a holon rests on, however far.

The two relations differ in shape. Within makes a tree, since a part has one whole. Upon makes a web, since a holon can stand on many others, and only one of them can be the whole it is part of. A solution stands on its requirements, and just as much on the goals, the background and the taste of the people who built it. Nesting shows within by itself. Every other connection has to be written, and that is why links exist.

A link is an offer, never a missing piece. The holon reads whole without it, and the sentence around it says what following it gives, so nobody follows blind. A link does not need a target that exists yet. A pointer to a source not yet read, or a question not yet answered, is a valid link: it is the holon nobody wrote. Written as a name and a year in brackets, it is a citation, and the same thing.

### 4.3 The knower

So far this section has said reader. The general word is knower: whoever comes to the knowledge, a person or a model in a session. Every knower is bounded, as [§2](#2-what-it-changes) says, and every knower moves through the holons in time. Comprehension is the knower's state: holding something well enough to act on it. Knowledge is comprehension written down so that it can form again in another knower, and the knower is what all of this is for.

The knower's motion has two directions. It expands, going into a holon's depth when a step of work needs the detail. It contracts, coming back to the surface when the step is done, and lets the depth go. Contracting loses nothing, because the depth stays at its address and the surface holds the point. This is what makes forgetting safe. Dropping context is not new. Dropping it and staying whole is.

What a knower holds at one moment is its prism. A glass prism lays light out by colour without losing any of it. This prism lays the field out by kind, and the knower takes the band the work at hand needs. For a person the prism is what they have read and still hold. For a model it is the context, taken under a contract: an agreement on what the session takes in and what it gives back. A prism is recomputed as the field moves, never taken once and kept.

### 4.4 The win

A win is what a knower gains from a holon against what reading it costs. It is what you understand after that you did not before, together with what that understanding is good for. Its size is not how much was said. It is comprehension gained per reading spent. A holon that gives no win is not knowledge yet, however true it is. It is material, waiting to be understood. And the one thing a holon holds is one win.

Wins are not equal. A few carry everything else, and most details matter only to their own subject. This inequality is the whole of the shape: the biggest win comes first, and each holon breaks down into smaller wins, down to the edge where the finest details wait for the few who need them. Place follows reach. A holon that many others stand on sits near the centre. One that few need sits at the edge.

The knower's order follows the same grade. Nothing arrives before its ground: every meaning is given before it is used, purpose before machinery, and every point where a knower may stop leaves them whole. Where a subject forces the other order, the debt is declared in a line. Borrowing is allowed. Silent borrowing is not.

### 4.5 Amplification

A knower standing in a field of holons leaves with more than it came with. That is what the medium does, and it is the reason to build one. Two knowers standing in the same field compound on each other the moment either writes, because what was written has an address and the other's prism picks it up. This is the change [§2](#2-what-it-changes) describes, seen from inside.

The medium amplifies error the same way. A wrong holon stood on by many spreads as fast as a right one. So the ground must stay true for whoever stands on it next, and that puts two duties on every writer.

Every fact keeps exactly one home. Copies are how ground rots: three copies of a rule quietly become three rules. So a fact is stated once and pointed at from everywhere else. And every claim carries its confidence in the writing, never left as a feeling. An unmarked guess, read a year later, becomes law without anyone deciding. Settled means settled for now, given what it stands on.

This is why the code cares about trust at all. It is not a rule laid on top. It is what amplification costs.

### 4.6 The trail

A write is a knower's act on the field, and every write is kept: a commit, addressed in time, holding exactly what changed. The sequence of a knower's writes is its trail, and the field keeps every trail. This is how the process of change is followed, not only its result. A commit is browsed by the same two relations as any holon, what it is within and what it stands on, so a change is read the way knowledge is read.

A field is held in a space, and a space says who may read it and who may write to it. A knower may keep a space of its own that nobody else reads. Publishing is a commit made visible to a wider space.

Whoever may read a space may comment in it, and a comment is a holon like any other: it stands on what it comments on, and it lives in the space shared by everyone who reads there. So a comment is a trail too, and trails can be walked by knower or by space: everything one person wrote, or everything said in one place.

Commits and comments are how a field grows past the head that started it. A written structure like this document is a simulation of the field, stewarded by these rules, and as it grows its line gets harder to hold in one head. Whatever shows the field, a page or a frontend, shows it by the same relations: a holon with what it is within, what it stands on, and what stands on it. That last one, the backlink, text cannot hold and the field can.
