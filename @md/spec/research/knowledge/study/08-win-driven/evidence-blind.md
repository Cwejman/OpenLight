# Evidence: the blind reconstruction

*Whether a heading carries its paragraph's win, measured 2026-09-05. Technique: a fresh session was given the ontology's headings alone, every h2 word and every h3 title with its links, and asked to write the paragraphs; a second fresh session graded each reconstructed paragraph against the original as match, partial or diverge. The skeleton and both reports are in git at 8ddd4b7, `passes/round-1/`. Strength: **measured**.*

## The numbers

Over 142 headings of the file as it stood before round one:

- Essences, 29: 9 match, 19 partial, 1 diverge. 96.6 percent carried.
- Relations, 113: 42 match, 60 partial, 11 diverge. 90.3 percent carried.
- All headings: 91.5 percent carried their paragraph's main point.

## What the divergences were

The eleven relation titles that misled fall into four shapes. Three stated the claim while the paragraph held the reason. Three were read from the wrong end of the relation, ground's "is stood on by briefs" among them. Three were puns that read two ways, "amplifies first" as build order, "the coined word" as etymology. Two were about why the heading was kept rather than what it says. The one essence that diverged was specification, whose rebuild-in-one-pass test is not recoverable from the word.

The blind writer flagged three of the twelve as underdetermined and wrote the other nine with confidence, so the writer could not tell which titles were misleading.

## What the titles hid

Facts derivable from no heading, that live only in paragraphs: ground's duty, one home per fact and every claim with its confidence; the rule that a linking title must read whole from the other end; the commit reciprocity rule; why a brief is rewritten whole; space as dimensions; the rulings and their dates. The reconstruction also added nine true sentences the original lacked, among them that the harness's bounding role passes to the boundary, kept by the kernel.

## What it grounds

The rule that a relation's title must read whole from the other end, recorded in the practice notes, holds at 91.5 percent and fails exactly where a title reads from its own side only. Round one retitled the divergent eleven by purpose and moved the hidden facts into titles or into the entries the facts belong to.

## What this does not reach

One blind writer and one grader, both models. Partial covers a wide band, from a missing example to a missing reason. The measure is of this file's titles, not of the rule in general; a second file written under the rule from the start is the next test.
