# Clean-room read of `ontology.md` (08-win-driven)

Read once, top to bottom, nothing else opened. Line numbers refer to the file as read.

## 1. What this is

A glossary of thirty terms for an unnamed project that wants to replace the "transcript plus harness" way of working with language models by a knowledge base of short, self-contained pieces ("briefs") joined by links, kept in git today and later in a typed, addressable store ("the field") governed by a small runtime ("the kernel"). Each term gets a one-paragraph essence, then a handful of h3 "relations" naming what it depends on. The document claims: comprehension per unit of reading is the only value; two laws (nothing before its ground; ground stays true) generate every other rule; a model session is nothing but what it has read, so the quality of models is mostly the quality of their ground; and once contracts live as data in the same store they govern, the "harness" as a separate technology disappears. It also records where its own words are unsettled and who ruled on what.

## 8. What is best

1. **"Is made by debriefing: conclusion to the surface, detail within"** (L29). The one relation that turns the whole scheme into a practice a reader can perform tomorrow: when work ends, push the conclusion up and leave the detail below, and the verbs are already in the language. Nothing else in the file is as actionable per line.
2. **"Is what a model is, since a fresh session holds nothing else"** (L89). Two sentences that explain why any of this matters for language models: a session is its ground, so the difference blamed on models is a difference in what they were given. It reframes "ground" from a writing rule into the lever on model quality.
3. **"Makes forgetting safe"** (L173). States what the medium buys the bounded reader: you may drop everything below the brief because the brief above holds the point and the link leads back. It ties knower, brief, link, and gradient together in four lines and names the actual product ("dropping it and staying whole").

Runner-up: **"Must stay true for readers not yet met, which is the second law"** (L93), for "three copies of a rule quietly become three rules", which is the sharpest single sentence in the file and, given section 7, the one the file most needs to obey.
