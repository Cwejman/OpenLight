## 7. The goal test

**Code half.** Entry why: "The working rules for how knowledge is written and arranged so that it reaches whoever needs it next." Order: Code, Comprehension, Knower, Win, Brief, Ground, Link, Holon, Gradient, Medium, Trail, Word, Amplify, Consolidation, Commit, Comment. The first seven follow the why: the value, who receives it, the unit, the unit of writing, what it stands on, how it joins. After that the order stops tracking the why. Holon (8) is Brief restated under an imported name and gains the reader nothing Brief did not. Trail (11) is stood on by the head, by Brief ("Is listed under its ground by its title") and by Ground, and comes after all of them; Word (12) is what the head says every entry is ("Each word is a brief") and is given after eleven words have been used. Amplify (13) and Consolidation (14) are both restatements: Amplify of Win and Ground, Consolidation of Brief, Commit and Amplify. Commit (15) is the only word in the half that tells a reader what to do ("Whoever makes a change commits it") and comes second to last. The order follows the why for about half its length. The word serving the goal least: **Consolidation**. Each of its three relations re-says another word ("Does for the medium what a brief does", "stands on one addressable state, a commit", "Is amplification seen from the field"), and its own text concedes the pairing is "reasoned" and the word "ruled".

**Platform half.** Entry why: "so that programs read and write what people and models read and write", restated as "Exists so that programs compound as knowers do". Order: Platform, Field, Citizen, Program, Contract, Substrate, Engine, Boundary, Prism, Space, Interface, Specification, Processor, Harness. Field, Citizen, Program follow the why. Contract (5) and Substrate (6) hold what the why actually turns on, "Keeps the law as data, which is the lever", and come after Program, which depends on both ("Is called by matching its contract"). Space (10) is stood on by Field ("every holon has an address"), Boundary ("a region of the space") and Holon in the other half ("placed in many dimensions"), and comes after all of them; the head allows this, but here the win is not small: without Space, "address" has no meaning for the first nine words. Prism (9) is about knowers, not programs ("The code's own word for the model's case is context"; "This is the one place the code treats the two knowers differently") and belongs in the code half by its own text. Interface (11) is "built next" yet eleventh. Processor (13) and Harness (14) are correctly last; the question is why they are present. The order follows the why for the first four words and then follows dependence loosely. The word serving the goal least: **Processor**. It states that it "Holds no comprehension" and "The win begins one layer up"; by the file's own unit it has no win, and nothing in either half stands on it.

## 8. Verdict

Not yet. The code half is close to specifiable: its value, unit, laws and units of writing are given once each and ordered by the why. The platform half is not: "law" carries four senses (the code's two laws, the contracts, the store's judging, permission), "run" is never given, three words are "named for now", the chunk/holon question is "held open", and the lever rests on the ambiguous word. The one change that would raise it most: give "law" one meaning, with its own word and home, and rename the other three senses. Every platform sentence that matters passes through it.
