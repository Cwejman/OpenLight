# Amnesia pass: `ontology.md`

Read once, top to bottom, with no other file open. Line numbers refer to the file as read.

## 1. What this is

A glossary of 29 terms, each an h2 with a one-line essence and a set of h3 "relations" linking to other terms. It serves a project whose name is never given (one mention of "OpenLight", line 409) that wants to replace the LLM "harness" (transcript-in, answer-out) with a shared written "medium": short self-contained prose pieces ("briefs") joined by links, stored as markdown in git, read by people and models alike, and later hosted by a runtime stack (kernel over substrate and processor, with programs and engines as "citizens"). The document's claim is that knowledge is measurable in "wins" (comprehension gained per reading spent), that briefs ordered by win form a "gradient" that keeps itself in order, and that a model reading from an addressed point in such a "field" gives repeatable answers where a transcript cannot. The document also declares its own governance: changes are ratified by git commits, not discussion.

## 4. Questions left open

1. What is "the code"? A document, a practice, a set of rules, a team? It is the most-used noun in the file and the one thing never defined. At line 529 it means source code.
2. What is the project? What is being built, for whom, and what exists today? "OpenLight" appears once (409) with no gloss. "The interface ... has to be built" (413) is the only statement of current status.
3. Who are the author and the steward? Is the steward a person or a model session? The document says sessions commit and ratify (3), which suggests a model, but never says.
4. How many laws are there? The "first law" (203) and "second law" (141) are named out of order and no list exists. Is "every relation has one home" a law?
5. What are the "six kinds" (5) the ontology was recut around, and the "five fundamentals"? Neither list appears. Prism "lays the field out by kind" (393): the same kinds?
6. What is a "point" in the field, concretely? A commit hash plus a path? A query? Without this, "the project's first claim" (305) and "makes inference deterministic" (95) cannot be evaluated.
7. Where do types live? Medium: "nothing typed ... No schemas, no edge types" (265). Field: "the medium given addresses and types" (299). Substrate: "types and boundaries, typed against itself" (441). Contract: "For the classical program, the contract is types" (485). Which is it, and at what layer does a schema first appear?
8. Was the order of this file measured or felt? Gradient says "centrality is measured, not felt" (217) by trail length. But Consolidation sits fifth with one inbound link (from Amplify), and Processor calls itself "Central" at position 24. Was trail length computed?
9. Why does the first entry stand on the second? The preamble says "what stands on a word follows it in the order" (5), yet Comprehension links forward to Knower, Amplify links forward to Brief, Ground, Medium and Consolidation, and Consolidation forward to Brief and Field. Either the rule or the order is wrong.
10. Why does "nothing is said twice" (5) hold when it does not? Line 13 and line 29 both define knowledge as comprehension that forms again in another knower. Line 321 and line 505 both say "Boundaries before a run, inspection during, history after." Line 117 and line 173 both describe the trail as the links read from the ground's side. Line 387 repeats line 385.
11. How does a model become a program (491) if a program cannot comprehend (45, 377)? Is a model-in-a-session a knower or a program or both?
12. What is a chunk, and how is it different from a holon other than "no epistemic duty" (453)? What is the epistemic duty of a holon, since Holon's entry mentions none?
13. What does the kernel add over an OS plus git? "Rebooted without loss", "the one thing that touches the disk", "judges every act" (425–429) describe any kernel with a filesystem and permissions. What is the specific claim?
14. What is the interface? A viewer, an editor, an IDE, a chat? "Where comprehension happens" (405) and "it has to be built to be felt" (413) are its only content.
15. Why is Harness, "Retired", the longest entry (seven relations) and last? If it is kept "so its absence is named" (565), why seven relations?
16. Is the holon etymology correct? "-on, part" (241) does not match Koestler's coinage (holos + -on as in proton). Who is cited?
17. Direction of fit [Searle 1979] (141): which direction is "confidence" and which is "status"? The sentence maps word-to-world/world-to-word onto describe/direct but does not say which is which.
18. What is "the pilot" (513)? The first user? A codename? An engine?
19. What is "purification" (333) and is it a routine operation or an accident?
20. What was ruled on 2026-09-05, in one place? Four entries cite the same day's rulings (5, 99, 289, 457, 525) but each gives a fragment.
21. Who is "we" (73)?
22. What does "elevated from this file" (5) mean for the edition, and what is the edition?

- "Is a [holon](#holon) with preceding matter" (531) — "preceding matter" is undefined. Better: "Is whole only within its goals, background and taste".
- "Is the study's second mark" (539) — private language. Better: "Is what the spec tree becomes when rewritten under the code".
- "Is left out with the transcript's other artefacts" (567) — the paragraph names compression and hand-over and says they dissolve. Better: "Takes compression and hand-over with it when it goes".

## 7. What is best

- **"Brief"** (103–125). The highest win in the file: a reader leaves knowing what the unit of writing is, how it is made (debriefed: conclusion up, detail within), that it must read whole without its links, and that the verbs are ordinary English ("brief me, debrief this session"). Every relation is one paragraph, none is provenance, and "The detail moves a level deeper, not away" (109) is the one sentence I would keep if the file were cut to one.
- **"Win"** (47–61). Three relations, three lines each, and the file's pricing rule falls out: "A long page that gives little is a small win; a line that changes what you can do is a large one" (53), and "A piece that gives no win is not knowledge yet, however true. It is material" (61). It is also the yardstick this pass used to rank the other 28, which is what a good early entry should do.
- **"Holon"** (223–241). Four relations, all short; gives surface/depth as "one holon seen twice" (231) and address as path (237), which is the vocabulary Brief, Commit, Consolidation and Gradient all lean on. The only entry whose every relation I could restate without the file. Its placement at 11 is the clearest evidence that the file's order was not made by the measure it names.
