## 3. What the titles hid

The 12 DIVERGE cases, grouped by what kind of fact the paragraph holds that no heading in the document lets a reader derive.

**The paragraph says why, the title says what.** The title states a claim; the paragraph holds the reason, and the reason is the win.

- Knower / Writes as well as reads: "Whoever makes a brief is bounded the same way as whoever reads it, so every limit on the reader binds the writer first, and the one who knows a thing stops being the only way to it once it is written."
- Brief / Can be a whole file or a section in one: "Everything it uses it explains or points out of itself, and following the pointer is optional."
- Specification / Is a holon with preceding matter: "The goals, the background and the taste it stands on are its larger wholes, and it is whole only in them."

**The title names a relation from one end; the paragraph reads it from the other.** The blind writer took the direction the title suggested; the original runs the opposite way.

- Ground / Is stood on by briefs: "Every brief lives twice, first handed to a reader, then as ground others stand on. The second life is where compounding leaves one head." (Blind read: every brief needs ground. Original: every brief becomes ground.)
- Comment / Is a trail too: "Walked by knower or by space, like any other write." (Blind read: a comment is a written trail. Original: a comment is a write, and a write leaves a derived trail.)
- Prism / Is cut from the field and loses none of it: "This one lays the field out by kind, and the knower takes the band the work at hand needs; the rest stays at its address." (Blind read: the prism is the whole field, not a selection. Original: it is a selection; "loses none" means the rest is still addressable.)

**The title is a pun or a compressed idiom that reads two ways.**

- Interface / Amplifies first: "Building it is where the arc goes next. Nobody has had the experience yet; it has to be built to be felt." ("First" is build order, not function priority.)
- Holon / Is the coined word for it: "Greek *holos*, whole, with the particle *-on*, part: the one word made for exactly this two-faced thing." ("Coined" means made from parts that mean whole-and-part, not coined by this document.)
- Substrate / Holds the chunk, which may become the holon: "Whole in itself, placed in others, with no epistemic duty. Renaming chunk to holon is an open ruling; the word is reserved for it." ("May become" is a pending rename, not a transformation.)

**The paragraph is meta: it says why the heading exists, not what the heading says.**

- Harness / Is superseded by the amplifier: "Kept in this ontology so its absence is named, and so the amplifier is read against what it replaces."
- Specification / Is the study's second mark: "The spec tree rewritten under the code: comprehensible, engaging, and still law-grade."

**The essence is a definition that the word plus its relations do not sum to.**

- Specification (essence): "the shortest text from which the code could be deleted and rebuilt in one pass, and no further."

## 6. Verdict

The skeleton carries the document: 130 of 142 headings gave a blind writer the paragraph's main point, and 51 gave it whole. What it does not carry is reasons, rulings, and duties: the twelve divergences are titles that state a claim whose paragraph holds the why, or that read one way from the brief and another from the ground. That is exactly the rule's failure mode. A title that reads whole only from its own side passed the blind test; a title that reads whole from the other end, as a sentence about the brief, is the one that would have carried the win. The rule is confirmed by where it was not followed.
