# Evidence: the address space of authors

*Each word of the ontology grounded in the author who named or first sharpened its notion, verified against a real source on the web, 2026-09-05. Technique: a fresh session read the ontology and searched the literature for each of the twenty-nine words, confirming title, year and claim before reporting. The full table with quotations and the departures is in [`passes/round-1/authors.md`](passes/round-1/authors.md). Strength: **observed**, each row verified once; the departures are reasoned.*

## Verified

Twenty-four of twenty-nine words carry a bracket in the ontology, each a link to a brief nobody has written:

- Comprehension, Kintsch 1988. Knower, Polanyi 1958. Amplify, Engelbart 1962, with Ashby 1956 behind it. Consolidation, Müller and Pilzecker 1900. Ground, Clark and Brennan 1991, with Harnad 1990. Link, Nelson 1965. Trail, Bush 1945. Gradient, Minto 1978. Holon, Koestler 1967. Word, Bloomfield 1926. Medium, McLuhan 1964. Field, Maxwell 1865. Commit, Gray 1981. Citizen, Aristotle. Prism, Newton 1672. Interface, Simon 1969. Kernel, Brinch Hansen 1970. Processor, von Neumann 1945. Contract, Meyer 1992. Program, McIlroy 1978. Engine, Babbage 1837. Specification, Parnas 1972. Resonance, under amplify, Grossberg 1976. Direction of fit, under ground, Searle 1979.

## None found

Brief has no author; the entry says everyday speech is its source, and the word's own history agrees, briefing being a 1910 usage popularised in wartime. Substrate has no computing author for a persistent store that outlives every kernel; Aristotle's substratum is the nearest figure and is not cited. Harness is trade usage with no origin. Space and comment have likely sources, Descartes and Bush, not cited as fits. Win has none; Engelbart's gaining comprehension to suit one's needs is the nearest.

## What the authors corrected

Bloomfield's minimum free form corrected word's essence from "below it nothing carries meaning" to "below it nothing stands alone". Bush's trail is stored and the ontology's is derived, and the entry now says so. Aristotle's citizen shares in decision, and the ontology admits programs, and the entry now says so. Brinch Hansen's nucleus is what can be extended with new systems, which is the kernel entry's replaceability. Gray's commit is atomic and durable, which is why the entry says one declaration kept whole rather than one write.

## The new address space

With brackets standing, the ontology's words are keys into the literature. A search for what the ontology stands on can be run against the world's writing and not only the project's: every use of Koestler's holon since 1967 with a Janus-faced whole, every gradient-first document form that cites Minto, every field that assigns a state to a point and every store that outlives its kernel. The author's phrase for it: connecting the ontology to the authors in the field gives web search a whole new address space.

## What this does not reach

Each row was verified once by one session; a person has not checked the sources. A citation is a link to a brief nobody has written, so following one gives a name and a year, not yet the brief.
