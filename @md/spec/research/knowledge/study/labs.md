# Where the search stands

## 1. The baseline pair

Labs 01 and 02 read the same forty-five reports of the August sweep for the two things a reading can be for. The first cut the corpus by subject, to inform, and the second walked it as a narrative, to engage. The first informed but did not engage; the second engaged.

Every later lab is read against them, and the difference in how the two land is the study's oldest evidence.

### 1.1 Lab 01 — the corpus cut categorically

The first reading of the August sweep, and the first structure ever built under the code. Forty-five research reports were read whole in one session and folded by subject, so that a reader looking for what the sweep knows about one territory finds it in one place. It was made to inform a decision rather than to be read through.

Its shape is itself an experiment, since nothing had been written under the code before it, and the code's open section was waiting on exactly this.

[The findings](01-categorical/output/README.md) are the piece: one brief per territory, each opening with what it settles and closing with what it does not establish. [The method](01-categorical/method.md) is how it was made, including the compression it achieved and what was deliberately left in the raw. It ran under [the edition of 2026-08-30](code-2026-08-30.md), which is why its links point there rather than at the law as it stands now.

### 1.2 Lab 02 — the same corpus as a walk

The second reading of the same forty-five reports, made to engage rather than to inform. Where lab 01 cut the material by subject, this one walks it: among the people, the works and the arguments the sweep found, in an order a reader follows rather than searches.

[The walk](02-encounters/output.md) is the piece. [The prompt](02-encounters/prompt.md) is what the writer was given, and with the edition it ran under it is the method record by construction.

## 2. Lab 10 — the code rewritten, and read

The lab that rewrote the law with the author rather than around a writer, and then put it to work on the hardest material the study has. The piece stands finished and unjudged, which was the whole point of running the lab.

Its edition was written section by section in dialog on 2026-09-10 and 11: the prose principle first, the gradient lifted to be the thesis, the two laws dropped as a frame, a section on what the code is for, the making raised from a principle to a section of its own, and a last section on the medium beyond prose, with every principle closing with how strongly it holds.

That edition is no longer here. It became the arc's standing law and lives at [the root](../code.md), with [the practice](../poc.md) and [the skill](../skill.md) beside it, because they govern the whole arc and not this lab.

The run is three prompts rather than one, and two stages. [The first](10-comprehension/prompt-1.md) compressed forty-five raw reports into [a corpus](10-comprehension/corpus/README.md) under the code, with fresh heads asked after each round only what the corpus had lost, four rounds in one session that held the raw throughout. [The second](10-comprehension/prompt-2.md) ran a fifth round in the lean shape, an orchestrator that never holds the raw and sends heads for what needs it. [The third](10-comprehension/prompt-3.md) wrote [the piece](10-comprehension/output/README.md) from the corpus. Its input differs from labs 08 and 09 by that corpus stage, and any comparison to the baseline pair must say so.

[The debrief](10-comprehension/debrief.md) is the run's record, kept current after every round, and it holds what the two shapes cost: the losses that mattered ran twelve, fifteen, three and six across the corpus rounds, converging and not converged. [The steps](10-comprehension/steps.md) are the line-per-step note the skill asks for, written before each step rather than after. [What the cold reads found](10-comprehension/feedback.md) is the record of the four fresh sessions sent at the edition, the practice and the two programs at the close.

[The author's note](10-comprehension/note.md) is begun and owed: where the making comes from, in the author's own words.

## 3. Lab 09 — the fixes lab 08 asked for

A rerun of lab 08 with one thing changed: the edition. Same prompt, same corpus, one serial run, so that what the fixes bought can be read against 08's output directly. The author's cold read of its output against 08's is to follow.

[The edition](09-gradient/code.md) carries the fixes. The order of a holarchy now names the level that was not broken down in both its costumes, the paragraph with bullets and the section that is not brief, pointed at the law and holding at every grain with no size attached. The ground gains the word for borrowed confidence. The practice cites sources known by number as links to their address, says the depth lean does not excuse a flat file, and no longer restates the code anywhere. And [the prompt](09-gradient/prompt.md) isolates the blank head from the repository's own bootstrap.

[The piece](09-gradient/output/README.md) is the output and [the debrief](09-gradient/debrief.md) holds its rounds and cost. [The practice](09-gradient/poc.md) is this lab's cut of it; the standing one is at [the arc's root](../poc.md).

## 4. Lab 08 — the first run under the cycle

The lab where the code became a document about wins, and the first run under the write-read-rewrite cycle the code's making section describes. It closes on its own feedback: the piece is flat and force-fed because the code never said nesting is how the gradient is made, the cost is the writer's context and the context is the judgment, and a word for borrowed confidence is owed. Those went into [lab 09](#3-lab-09--the-fixes-lab-08-asked-for). The author's request, the engagement angle, returns in a later run there.

Its edition was written with the author in dialog through 2026-09-09: the why, what it changes, then what knowledge is made of under two laws, the brief holding its contract against the win, the two forces as the thesis, why-first, the holarchy and the link, then the ground. Five principles follow as concepts, each closing with how strongly it holds.

[The edition](08-win-driven/code.md) is that law and [the prompt](08-win-driven/prompt.md) is the run's mechanics. [The piece](08-win-driven/output/README.md) is what came out, written by one writer over three rounds with two blank heads until it converged; two earlier writers under the first wording of the cycle principle skipped it entirely, and their reports are in git.

[The debrief](08-win-driven/debrief.md) holds the rounds and the run's cost. [What the run teaches](08-win-driven/feedback.md) holds the three things it asked for. [The markdown practice](08-win-driven/poc.md) began here as a first cut, and now stands at [the arc's root](../poc.md).

[The ontology](08-win-driven/08-01-ontology/README.md) was to fill the edition's fourth section and was set aside by the author on 2026-09-07. It stands whole with its evidence, to be rerun once the code is lived.

## 5. Lab 07 — the breakdown, tested

The one lab that tests a candidate rule rather than adopting one, and the only one so far to measure rather than judge. The structure paid: one level deeper, with the content held fixed, reads 46% shorter at the level a reader stops while holding more. The rule it tested is the code's order principle now, and carries that measurement as its strength.

A read of lab 06's output found one shape recurring: a slim surface followed by bold-led bullets, at every heading level. That is a brief whose nested briefs never got their headings, so every win arrives at one depth in one serving. This lab reworked that output one level deeper with the content held fixed, so the only variable is the breakdown.

Because the rule was under test rather than adopted, it lived in [the prompt](07-breakdown/prompt.md) and not in [the edition](07-breakdown/code.md). A rule a lab may reject must not enter the law first, and that is the exception [the method](method.md#1-what-a-lab-ships) names.

[The reworked piece](07-breakdown/output.md) is the output. The measurement, and the four things it does not reach, are in [`evidence-breakdown.md`](07-breakdown/evidence-breakdown.md); [`evidence-unit-of-change.md`](07-breakdown/evidence-unit-of-change.md) grounds the rule beside it. The two are the pattern every later lab's evidence is meant to follow.

## 6. Lab 06 — the code as the instruction

The lab that changed how a lab is delivered. Until here the prompt carried the rules and the code stood behind it. Here the prompt went bare and a full edition of the code became the writer's primary instruction, so that what the writer followed and what the study believed were the same document. Its output is the leanest reading yet.

That edition is also where the unit was renamed. Fold gave way to brief, and the handing register that came with it, to brief and to debrief, entered the law.

[The edition](06-principles/code.md) is the law this lab ran under and the first to be written as one. [The prompt](06-principles/prompt.md) carries only mechanics, which is the point. [The piece](06-principles/output.md), *The ground others hold*, runs about 5,200 words.

The pattern this lab's output showed, one shape repeating at every heading level, is what [lab 07](#5-lab-07--the-breakdown-tested) was cut to test.

## 7. Structure without narrative

Labs 03, 04 and 05 took the narrative away and tested structure on its own, one rule more each time. The one verdict so far, partial, is on 04: writing from the raw alone caps the depth a writer can give.

### 7.1 Lab 03 — importance first, and nothing else

The first of the three. It gave the writer a single rule: the biggest understanding comes first, and each thing after it stands on what came before.

Nesting was not asked for and the spine rules were not given. What the lab isolates is whether ordering by importance, by itself, carries a reader who has no story to follow.

[The piece](03-importance-first/output.md) is the output, and [the prompt](03-importance-first/prompt.md) is what produced it.

### 7.2 Lab 04 — importance first, with nesting

The second of the three, and the first to ask for depth. It repeats lab 03's rule and adds one thing: what the biggest understanding did not need goes beneath it, in parts of its own.

So the pair 03 and 04 isolates nesting. Same corpus, same absence of narrative, one rule apart.

[The piece](04-importance-first-nested/output.md) is the output, and [the prompt](04-importance-first-nested/prompt.md) is what produced it.

### 7.3 Lab 05 — the full spine

The third and last. Where 03 gave one rule and 04 gave two, this one gave the writer the whole spine the code had reached by then, so that what the earlier two isolated could be seen working together.

[The piece](05-spine/output.md) is the output, and [the prompt](05-spine/prompt.md) is what produced it.
