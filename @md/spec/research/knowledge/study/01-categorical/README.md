# Lab 01 — the corpus cut categorically

The first reading of the August sweep, and the first structure ever built under the code. Forty-five research reports were read whole in one session and folded by subject, so that a reader looking for what the sweep knows about one territory finds it in one place. It was made to inform a decision rather than to be read through.

Its shape is itself an experiment, since nothing had been written under the code before it, and the code's open section was waiting on exactly this.

[The findings](output/README.md) are the piece: one brief per territory, each opening with what it settles and closing with what it does not establish. [The method](method.md) is how it was made, including the compression it achieved and what was deliberately left in the raw.

It ran under [the edition of 2026-08-30](../code-2026-08-30.md), which is why its links point there rather than at the law as it stands now.
