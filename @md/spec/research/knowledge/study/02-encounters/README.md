# Lab 02 — the same corpus as a walk

The second reading of the same forty-five reports, made to engage rather than to inform. Where lab 01 cut the material by subject, this one walks it: among the people, the works and the arguments the sweep found, in an order a reader follows rather than searches.

The two together are the baseline pair. Every later lab is read against them, because between them they hold the two things a reading can be for, and the difference in how they land is the study's oldest evidence.

[The walk](output.md) is the piece. [The prompt](prompt.md) is what the writer was given, and with the edition it ran under it is the method record by construction.
