# Lab 10 — the code rewritten, and read

The lab that rewrote the law with the author rather than around a writer, and then put it to work on the hardest material the study has. Its edition was written section by section in dialog on 2026-09-10 and 11: the prose principle first, the gradient lifted to be the thesis, the two laws dropped as a frame, a section on what the code is for, the making raised from a principle to a section of its own, and a last section on the medium beyond prose, with every principle closing with how strongly it holds.

That edition is no longer here. It became the arc's standing law and lives at [the root](../../code.md), with [the practice](../../poc.md) and [the skill](../../skill.md) beside it, because they govern the whole arc and not this lab.

The run is three prompts rather than one, and two stages. [The first](prompt-1.md) compressed forty-five raw reports into [a corpus](corpus/README.md) under the code, with fresh heads asked after each round only what the corpus had lost, four rounds in one session that held the raw throughout. [The second](prompt-2.md) ran a fifth round in the lean shape, an orchestrator that never holds the raw and sends heads for what needs it. [The third](prompt-3.md) wrote [the piece](output/README.md) from the corpus.

[The debrief](debrief.md) is the run's record, kept current after every round, and it holds what the two shapes cost: the losses that mattered ran twelve, fifteen, three and six across the corpus rounds, converging and not converged. [The steps](steps.md) are the line-per-step note the skill asks for, written before each step rather than after. [What the cold reads found](feedback.md) is the record of the four fresh sessions sent at the edition, the practice and the two programs at the close.

[The author's note](note.md) is begun and owed: where the making comes from, in the author's own words.
