# Writing knowledge down, weighed against the evidence

There is a [code for writing knowledge down](../code.md) — knowledge laid as a gradient of small briefs, each readable alone, nested and linked, one home per fact, confidence written in the text — and a wider claim that the same shape should be taken past prose into the medium beneath, so that programs meet by matching contracts rather than by hand-written glue. This piece weighs that position against the evidence, and the verdict is that three of its five claims are unvalidated rather than refuted, one is barely examined, and the fifth survives narrowly — while the materials the code is built from, stories and hierarchy and signals and prose and plain files, are the ones with evidence behind them.

The evidence is a sweep — the word is used throughout for that one exercise, its *lanes* are its territories, and *the raw* is the forty-five reports themselves: on 2026-08-26, forty-five research agents each read a territory at source and returned one report. Eighteen of them were briefed by the project and twenty-seven were sub-agents those eighteen spawned, so the forty-five are not forty-five independent observations, and where this piece says a finding recurs, that is convergence across a partly shared lineage. Those reports were debriefed into [the material](../corpus/README.md), about ninety thousand tokens, and this piece is debriefed from that, which is the code's word for what happens when a stretch of work ends: the conclusions go to the surface and the detail stays beneath. Three things about it govern everything below, and [what this rests on](ground.md) holds them in full. The session that wrote this checked nothing against a primary source, so every mark here is borrowed twice over. The italic line closing a section is that mark, in the code's terms of measured, seen, reasoned or preferred, often with the source's own grade carried alongside it: *paper-only* means a real published result with nothing deployed behind it, *opinion* means an assertion whoever made it, *observational* means seen rather than measured under intervention, and *carried* means taken from the material without independent check. And the sweep was commissioned against an earlier statement of this project's rules rather than against the five claims as [what is being weighed](claims.md) states them, so what follows are answers to a related question rather than to exactly this one.

## 1. What is being weighed

The claims come from the code as it stood on 2026-09-11, and they are stated before any evidence arrives so that a reader can tell what is being argued about: the gradient, the brief as a checkpoint, one home per fact with confidence written, the shape of the making, and the medium that reaches past prose. [What is being weighed](claims.md).

## 2. What the evidence did to each claim

This is the answer to the question the piece asks, and a reader who wants only that can stop after it. Taken one claim at a time: the gradient has no measurement of its own, and what evidence touches it is mixed — one result against its opening that lands on a failure mode the code already instructs against, one in favour of putting the governing understanding at the top, and three converging against going deep, which is the sharpest thing said against it. The checkpoint is untested. One home per fact has lost the justification usually given for it. The making is barely touched. And the medium claim survives in the form the code states; what the evidence refutes is a stronger form the code does not hold, which is worth knowing because the refutation is often quoted against it. Against that, the evidence supports five things the code does without having had to argue for them, and one thing the market decided — and the heaviest single argument against arrangement as a lever is left standing rather than answered. *Reasoned, by this piece, from findings carried second-hand; no verdict here is itself a measurement.* [What the evidence did to each claim](verdict.md).

The sections below are the same evidence cut the other way, by territory rather than by claim, and each is the entrance to the briefs that hold it. They are where the verdict's sentences come from.

## 3. The practice has no authority to lend

Every established practice for writing knowledge down rests on one document that everybody cites, and in every case that document is one person's account of what worked for them. Where anyone later measured, the result was null or small, and it lands on the most-adopted conventions in the field.

So you cannot borrow authority from the practice, because it has none to lend — and the mechanism by which this field's circulating numbers came to exist is worth knowing, because the same three mechanisms produced nearly all of them. [The catalogue, and how the numbers came to exist](practice.md).

## 4. The levers that replicate are not the ones the practice argues about

Three text-design effects replicate: cue words that announce structure, breaking material into units the reader paces, and narrative, which is the largest effect in the literature — though its figures are the one place in this folder where the sweep never opened the paper. Nothing identifies an optimal length, and the numbers quoted for one come from experiments that fixed the item count and could not have found anything else. On depth the literature is not silent: three results from three directions converge against going deep, and they are gathered in [what the evidence did to each claim](verdict.md) §1.

Two findings bound how much any of it matters. Where professionals have been observed, the dominant failure in a long document is finding the right part rather than understanding it — and the field that exists to solve finding has the worst evidence base in this material. [What a reader is measured to get](reading/README.md).

## 5. What is known about the making

Review works at a defensible base rate, and every refinement of how to review returns nulls. The fresh reader with no history of the work is measured exactly once, raising error detection from 24.6% to 28.6% — a real gain at an absolute level low enough to be its own warning. And nothing in the sweep addresses whether writing a thing whole survives a corpus larger than one rewrite can hold.

[What is known about the making](making.md). *One measurement, one absence, and a set of review verdicts carried at one remove — their verdicts reached the sweep but not the studies behind them, so they cannot be checked from this material.*

## 6. A document lives only as long as something consumes it

Artifacts that generate something downstream — code, tests, other artifacts — are kept current, and artifacts that only describe are not. That one distinction accounts for every decay figure in this material without needing another mechanism.

It lands on this code too, because prose knowledge is descriptive by construction. [What keeps a document alive](survival.md). *One study behind the distinction, self-reported, in one industry; four measured corpus studies behind the decay figures; the application to prose knowledge is this piece's own and untested.*

## 7. Better writing has a ceiling, and the receiver sets it

When knowledge fails to move between people, the barriers that dominate are the receiver's capacity to absorb it, the ambiguity of the knowledge itself, and how arduous the relationship between the parties is. Motivation ranks low, and so does prose quality — though the ceiling is weakest in high-technology work, which is where a software project sits.

Nothing there says writing does not matter — the strongest measured result in this whole material is a document-design one, where a stripped, task-focused manual cut learning time by 40%, in two small experiments whose meta-analysis was never peer-reviewed. It says the return on better writing is bounded by who is receiving it, and that a document cannot repair a misunderstanding in the moment. [Where better writing stops helping](ceiling.md). *Measured once, well, and never replicated, for the barriers; measured in two experiments of 19 and 32 people, and replicated once, for the manual.*

## 8. Structure pays where a claim is checked by someone who was not there

Typing something — a field with a declared meaning, a schema, a description attached to a tool, a permission rule — pays where a claim must be evaluated by a party who was not present when it was written, under an asynchronous or adversarial relationship, with a consequence for being wrong. Prose wins where the payload is read once and judged by the reader on the spot.

One gate overrides all of it: if the type would have to be inferred rather than observed or declared, do not type it, because incomplete prose fails visibly and a wrong type answers. [Where structure pays, and the gate](structure/README.md).

## 9. If you mean to type the medium

Every part of a typed, versioned, permission-governed substrate has been built, several parts repeatedly, and the combination nowhere. The graveyard is more instructive than the survivors, and the plainest fact in it is that plain Markdown files became a moat in 2026 because coding agents can read them.

Three things break when such a system runs: schema change, transitive permission, and deriving the interface from the data. [The substrate question](medium/README.md).

## 10. What nobody has measured

Five absences matter more than most of the findings. Nobody has raced human-declared structure against a well-written prose document on the same corpus with the same reader, which is the comparison the whole medium claim turns on. Nobody has varied a document set's organisation while holding its content constant. Nobody has studied a professional's use of a document over months. Nothing bounds how large a body of knowledge can get. And four of this code's rules have no direct experimental test at all — a piece written under all four cannot be neutral about them. [The missing studies](unknown.md).
