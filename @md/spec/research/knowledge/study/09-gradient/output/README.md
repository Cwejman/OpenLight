# What the sweep leaves standing

On 2026-08-26 eighteen research agents, and twenty-seven they spawned, swept the web for what the world already knows about the claims [the code](../code.md) makes, about the numbers its earlier editions leaned on, and about the plan its last section reasons toward. Read this and you will know which of those claims the world backs, which numbers you may no longer cite, and where the plan meets ground that has already been tried. It is written for whoever holds the code and the plan, to decide what the next edition says and what gets built first; anyone joining the work can read it for the same reason. The forty-five reports in [`raw/`](../../../../sweep-2026-08/raw/) are the ground. Every claim here carries the grade its agent gave it and was not checked again.

## 1. What was tested

Three things were put to the world, and the parts below name them by these words. The code is the document beside this one: two laws and a few principles for knowledge written as prose in briefs. The material behind the code is what the code replaced: earlier editions that carried a ladder of fifteen rules and a chart of eleven territories, resting on numbers borrowed from the science of memory and from software folklore, seven plus or minus two, four chunks, two levels, ten to one, one to ten to a hundred. The code dropped the numbers and kept the shape; this debrief still checks the numbers, because they circulate wherever knowledge structure is argued and the reasons for them still stand behind some of the code's rules. Seen, in the sweep's own coverage map of that material.

The plan is what the code's fifth section reasons toward and does not name: a medium in which a program, a screen, a person and a model are all citizens, a program declaring what it takes and gives as structure, a screen declared over the same data, a model's context selected from the medium and its output landing back as structure. The sweep's commission had sketched the plan further than the code does, as typed records carrying contracts owned by whoever minted them, nothing transitive, permission written in the query language and filtering everything uniformly, and every write a commit on a branch. Where a part says "the plan," it means that sketch. Seen, in the briefs the agents were given, as the reports quote them.

## 2. The win

Picture the reader the code was written for, a new colleague or a fresh session, opening a body of knowledge with a task in hand. They can read every sentence in it. What decides whether they act well is whether they find the part that bears on the task, and whether the door to it said what was behind it, because a door that does not say is a door they never open. That is what the sweep found wherever someone measured what helped a reader: the shape the code asks for is backed by the world, but only its navigational half. A hierarchy of headings rather than a web of links; each piece carrying the chain of headings above it; a pointer to the right file rather than an explanation of the repository; a recommendation specific enough to act on. This holds for a person and it holds for a model, and it is the one place the two agree. Reasoned, from [what is known about a reader](readers.md) and [what a model reads](models.md).

The numbers are gone. Each of the figures the material behind the code leaned on is a coincidence its author disowned, a limit measured under conditions a reader never meets, an optimum at one fixed item count, a training unit's slogan, or one author's memory of watching an editor scroll. Measured, by the agent who chased each to its source; [the numbers brief](numbers.md) gives the sentence to use instead of each.

The hope is unbacked. The hope is that a better document changes an outcome on its own, and in forty years there is one experimental programme for it, on word-processor manuals in the 1980s, and a handful of small trials. The largest survey series retracted its own multiplier. Documents that only describe rot, while the ones that generate something get kept. When developers were asked what got them productive, a mentor came before the document. Measured, in the reports [what a document changes](documentation.md) stands on.

Where prose and structure meet, the world has an answer the plan must take: type the edges, keep the body prose. Every system in the sweep that typed identity, address, permission, provenance, freshness and a small declared vocabulary is alive; every one that typed the content, the editor, the notes, the schema of the body, died, was walked back, or shipped too late. A type a model infers is worse than prose, because it answers with confidence where thin prose looks thin. Measured and shipped, in the reports [the medium](medium.md) and [the interface](interface.md) stand on.

The pieces the plan needs beyond that are each unsolved in anything that ships: a context that can be branched and replayed, a permission that filters search and counts the same way it filters bodies, a schema that merges. Shipped-absent, in the same reports.

The sweep has two cautions of its own, and they are carried here. Most agents ran out of web search and fell back to fetching pages directly, so sources with open access and an API, W3C, GitHub, arXiv, PyPI, are over-represented and blogs and talks under-represented. And two agents caught automated summarisers inventing quotations and sample sizes, so a figure is worth what its agent's own verification note says. Where an agent could not verify, this says so too.

## 3. How to read the grades

The grades are the sweep's, not this debrief's, and each claim below keeps the one its agent gave. Measured means a study or a live count the agent read itself. Meta-analysed means many studies pooled; paper-only means a study or a pooling not yet lived with, and the two words are joined where both hold. Observational means seen in the field without a control. Shipped means a thing running in the world, and adopted or niche says how widely; announced means promised and not running; a research prototype is running only where it was built; shipped-absent means the thing is missing from everything that runs. Stated means a vendor's or an author's own figure; opinion means someone's say-so; folklore means a number with no source behind it; partly traceable means a real source at the start of the chain that the agent could not read. Unverified means the agent named it and could not reach it. Where this debrief joins findings into a claim of its own, it says reasoned, and says from which reports. Nothing here was checked against its source a second time, so a wrong grade in a report is a wrong grade here.

Effect sizes are given the way the studies give them. A d is a difference measured in standard deviations, so 0.2 is small and 0.8 large; a g is the same with a small-sample correction; an f is the same idea for a comparison of more than two groups, 0.4 being large; an r is a correlation, from 0 for none to 1 for perfect; an odds ratio below 1 means the outcome became rarer. A p is the chance of seeing the result with nothing behind it, and .05 is the usual bar.

## 4. The parts, and what each gives

The parts are laid in the order they stand on each other. The first clears the numbers, so the second can say what the science backs on clean ground; the third asks what a document changes, standing on what a reader is; the fourth carries the same questions to a model; the last two take the plan beyond prose to the medium and to the screen. Each is whole, opens with what it gives, and closes with where it stands and what its reports could not establish; a reader who wants only one may open only that one.

[The numbers you may no longer cite](numbers.md) gives, for each figure, what its source says and a sentence to use instead. [What is known about a reader](readers.md) gives the code's shapes the science backs, hierarchy, signalling and story, and the finding that professionals fail with a long document at finding rather than at understanding. [What a document changes](documentation.md) gives the near-absence of causal evidence for documentation, the barriers that stop knowledge travelling, and the fate of decision records and documentation systems. [What a model reads](models.md) gives the model's wall, the navigational half that helps it, the explanatory prose that does not, and how the harness moved. [What may be typed, and what must stay prose](medium.md) gives the verdict on typing knowledge with its evidence from the semantic web's lineage, the systems that version structure, and permission. [What a screen driven from data costs](interface.md) gives what happened to everyone who tried it, and what a contract must carry.

## 5. Open

The sweep leaves the code's own questions open, and adds sharper ones. The study that would settle the plan's central bet has not been run, and [the medium brief](medium.md#1-type-the-edges-keep-the-body-prose) says which. No study anywhere has documentation as the manipulated variable and a real outcome as the measure, outside the 1980s. No study follows professionals re-reading a long document over months. Whether skipping substitutes for prose forked by audience is untested, and so is any size rule for a brief. Whether a story helps a reader who is a model has not been asked. And whether an agent can branch a context safely is stated as a problem and solved nowhere, as [the models brief](models.md#5-how-the-harness-moved) says. Each part closes with what its own reports could not establish.
