# The project it began in

## 1. What OpenLight is

[OpenLight](https://github.com/Cwejman/OpenLight) is the project the study began in. It builds a medium in which knowledge, programs and interfaces live as typed, versioned structure, so that a model completes from a point in that medium rather than from a pasted transcript.

That medium is what OpenLight calls the substrate: pieces of structure, their connections and their types, every change a commit. Where the corpus and the labs speak of a substrate, they mean this or the idea of it. Its first version, the pilot, is specified, and its code follows the specs.

*Carried from [OpenLight's README](https://github.com/Cwejman/OpenLight/blob/main/README.md), 2026-09-14; not checked against its specs here.*

## 2. Why the search began there

OpenLight's specs were written in the order things are defined rather than the order they are understood, and its README says so: how knowledge is best structured, for people and agents alike, was not solved. The study is that search.

What it found, the code, took the place of the knowledge section in [OpenLight's conventions](https://github.com/Cwejman/OpenLight/blob/main/@md/conventions.md#knowledge-structure), which is the first mark on [the study's finish line](README.md#1-the-finish-line).

*Seen, in OpenLight's README and conventions, 2026-09-14.*

## 3. The sweep was sent for two things

The sweep of 2026-08-26 was commissioned in one message that asked two things: where the world stands on knowledge structure, and where OpenLight stands among the projects near it. So the corpus holds territories that only the second question explains, the typed substrates and the interfaces built from schemas. [The sweep's own record](sweep-2026-08/method.md) quotes the commission whole.

*Seen, in the sweep's record, 2026-09-14.*

### 3.1 The map of where OpenLight stands stays there

The second question was also answered in [OpenLight's landscape](https://github.com/Cwejman/OpenLight/blob/main/@md/spec/research/landscape.md), a map of the projects near it drawn from the same sweep. It is OpenLight's own, so it stays there and is linked from the study.

*Seen, 2026-09-14.*

## 4. The spec tree in the finish line

The study's second mark, the spec tree rewritten under the code, means [OpenLight's spec tree](https://github.com/Cwejman/OpenLight/tree/main/@md/spec): the pilot's substrate, engine, view and the rest. The mark is out of date, and it stays as the study wrote it.

The author's direction since is that OpenLight declares that tree a legacy artefact and writes it again under the rule, as [this repository's next steps](../repository.md#7-what-comes-next) say.

*The mark is the study's, as written; the direction the author's, 2026-09-14.*
