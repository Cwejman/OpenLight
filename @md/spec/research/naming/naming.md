# Naming — the search for what the system is called

*Research, 2026-08-23. Seeded from the framing exercise ([`framing.md`](../framing.md)) at the author's direction: the name comes after the capability to frame, so every candidate answers to the ladder there. Nothing is ruled; **OpenLight** stands until a name earns succession. The author's read of the incumbent: it does not hold what the system really is.*

## What the name must do

- Survive the sentence rung: "*X* is a compute environment where …" — the name pulls weight, not rides along.
- Sit inside one of the tension pairings (substrate × surface · typed × lived · knowledge × compute · medium × environment · place × completion · field × citizen · commit × compound) rather than beside them.
- Leave room to grow: knowledge is the first tenant, not the last — a name bound to documents, notes, or AI ages badly.
- Practicals, checked late but before any adoption: collision search, domain, pronounceability across languages, how it shortens in speech (people will say something two-syllable regardless).

## Directions, by pairing

- **field / place** — Field, Openfield, Locus. Plain, spatial, matches "completion from a place". Field alone is likely uncollectable as a name; qualified forms get heavy.
- **ground / medium** — Loam, Ground, Bedrock. The compounding-substrate feel; loam is alive where bedrock is inert — aliveness is the right connotation for a medium things grow in.
- **joining** — Loom, Ply, Splice. Composition-forward. (*Weft* caught the author's eye elsewhere first — [`weft.md`](../weft.md), another project in an adjacent space, not a peer of this one; the word is not forbidden, but the collision is real and documented.)
- **growth / compounding** — Coppice, the mycelium family. Organic compounding; trend-scent risk.
- **light** (the incumbent) — OpenLight: openness and clarity, carries the project's history; says nothing of field, type, or compute.

## Round one — breadth (2026-08-23, steward sweep)

Nine meaning-fields swept for candidates; each survivor tested in the sentence rung and collision-checked. Gut decides; this is the breadth to feel through.

**Tier 1 — strong matches.**

- **Topos** *(Greek: place; rhetoric: the commonplaces knowledge is drawn from; mathematics: a typed universe rich enough to reason inside)*. The triple meaning is almost unfair: *completion from a place*, knowledge's own storehouse, and a typed world — all in one word. Sentence rung: "Topos is a compute environment where …" — the name does half the sentence's work. Risks: academic scent; topos theory is a live math field (a flattering, non-product collision).

- **Mylla** *(Swedish: living topsoil — and the verb: to work something into the soil)*. The living ground where what falls compounds into fertility, and the act of folding-in — both estates of the compounding story in a homely word. Nordic identity travels well (the Spotify/Klarna precedent); ownable, pronounceable abroad. Sentence rung: carries ground and growth; compute rides the sentence, not the name.

- **Locus** *(Latin: place; mathematics: the set of points satisfying an expression; genetics: an addressable position)*. A locus is literally what a selection is — the place an expression describes. Clean, short, serious. Collisions: Locus Magazine, Locus Robotics — distant fields.

- **Glänta / Glenta** *(Swedish: a clearing in the forest; the verb: to open a door ajar)*. The lit, open place inside dense wood — continuity with OpenLight's light, plus place, plus the gentlest open. Collision: *Glänta*, a Swedish literary journal — same cultural sphere, worth weighing.

**Tier 2 — real, with a catch.**

- **Loam** — alive medium, short, warm; says ground, not compute.
- **Manifold** — *many folds*; a space locally simple everywhere — the knowledge thesis in one mathematical word. Collisions: the car part, Manifold Markets.
- **Lattice** — the hidden aptness: union, intersection, subtraction *is* lattice algebra — the boundary language's own mathematics. Collision: Lattice (HR software).
- **Plenum** — the fully-filled space *and* the assembly that governs; field + governance in one. Institutional scent.
- **Källa** *(Swedish: a spring — and a cited source)*. Provenance and living origin in one word; the umlaut is a real barrier abroad.

**Benchmarks, dead on collision** — named so the meaning is kept even where the word is lost: **Confluence** (flows joining into one — exactly the meaning wanted; Atlassian owns it in this very space) · **Foundry** (where type is cast — the *typed* pun; Palantir owns it) · **Commons** (the governed shared ground; Creative/Wikimedia own it) · **Weft** ([`weft.md`](../weft.md)).

**Fields swept with nothing surviving**: water beyond källa/confluence (delta, ström), mythic names (Mnemosyne-class — too heavy), invented blends (all read as startups), civic beyond plenum/commons (agora — taken; forum, torg — market scent), type-craft beyond foundry (ligature — the medical sense; sort, matrix — taken or confusing).

**Steward's read, gut withheld**: Topos and Mylla pull hardest from opposite poles — Topos from the claim (place, type, knowledge), Mylla from the value (aliveness, compounding, folding-in). Locus is the safe strong middle. Glänta is the one that keeps OpenLight's soul.

## The compounding critique (2026-08-24)

The author questioned the description's central verb: *compound* reads as mere merging, where the intended meaning is **value exceeding the pieces**. Talked through: "compound" is two words — the chemical sense (put together, a mixture) is merging; the financial sense (interest on interest, growth on ground of prior growth) is exactly the intended mechanism, and the README uses it — but a reader can't be trusted to receive that sense. The intended property has names in three registers: *emergence* (worn thin), *superadditivity* (exact, cold), *increasing returns / recombinant growth* (economics of ideas — the closest term of art, since the claim is that **the value lives in the joins, and joins outnumber pieces**). Statically the whole exceeds the parts; temporally that same fact *is* compounding — one phenomenon, two views. Working resolution, open until the description is next reworked: state the mechanism once in plain sight (*"each piece raises the value of every piece already there"* / *"the joins are worth more than the pieces"*) and let "compound" serve as shorthand after.

Consequence for naming: the walks had already converged on more-than-sum symbols without the concept being named — Braid ×2, Reef ×3, Comb ×2 among the keepers. **The steer for round five**: words where the whole-exceeding-the-parts, or the medium that makes it happen, is legible on first hearing — no footnote; plus the second lane the author named: black-and-white, crystal-clear English, the *Openfield* pattern. (Openfield itself checked 2026-08-24: taken several times over — openfield.ai organizing platform, the British grain co-op, an EdTech UX firm; OpenFOAM even expands to "Open Field Operation And Manipulation.")

The order stands: the name must survive being spoken bare; the domain follows by one of three routes — a rare clear .com, a category gTLD, or a ccTLD.

## The gate (2026-08-24, the author's round-five reaction)

The plain-compound lane is ruled out wholesale: Seedbed, Watertable and their class fail — *"you can't call an application that"* — a common noun-phrase never detaches from its everyday meaning. The author's named benchmark: **Zed**, the IDE — short, rolls right, feels right; a letter-name is already a name, and it succeeds while carrying almost no meaning at all. What every walk has been converging on, now stated as law: **namehood and mouth-feel are the gate; meaning is the chooser among what passes.** The test is the running sentence — "open X," "built on X," "runs in X" — and the name must detach cleanly from common usage. Passes: rare-but-real words (the author on round five: **Wax** and **Wain** roll nicely; **Ink** short and nice, crowded, open to it), name-shaped Norse words, coinages with the right mouth (Velm). Fails: everyday noun-phrases and kitchen-common nouns, regardless of meaning-fit. **Length is not the gate** (author, same day): **Obsidian** is the counter-benchmark — three syllables and a good brand; a name-shaped polysyllable (a substance, a stone, a made thing) passes where a kitchen-common monosyllable fails. The earlier "one or two syllables" taste line is hereby loosened to a lean, not a law.

**The arc re-read through the gate** — what stands from every round once namehood is required: the standing seven (Kelda · Terrane · Mycel · Operad · Fold · Kiln · Geode) all pass; round two/three's Zed-class keepers (Vang · Velm · Tarn · Holm · Kevel · Kedge · Skep ×3 · Stell ×3); round four's sound-pluses (Tar · Koji · Kelp · Lya · Umber · Cove · Ark); round five through the gate: Wax · Wain · Ink · Yoke · Quire · Shoal · Firth · Berth · Marl · Sinter · Fount.
