# Round five — more-than-sum, said plain

*Three generators under the two rulings of 2026-08-24: the compounding critique — the value lives in the joins, so the name should carry whole-exceeds-parts legibly on first hearing, no footnote — and the author's second lane, black-and-white crystal-clear English (the Openfield pattern; Openfield itself is taken). Lanes: more-than-sum symbols · clear compounds · plain medium words. 160 unique names after dedup against rounds one to four and the hand-rounds. × marks cross-generator convergence. Standouts were live-searched; verdicts sit in their brackets. ⚠ marks a fatal or near-fatal owner.*
