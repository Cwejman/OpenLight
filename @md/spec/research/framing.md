# Framing — holding the system in few words

*Research, 2026-08-23, opened at the author's direction: the name comes after the capability to frame — sentences and word-pairings that hold everything precede a good name. Nothing here is ruled; the current name (OpenLight) stands until a framing earns its successor. The author's own habitual phrase: "a compute environment."*

## The ladder

Each rung must be true alone; shorter buys reach, longer buys precision.

- **Two words.** *The field.* — what everything else is said against. (Runner-up: *a typed field*; *one medium*.)

- **A phrase.** *A compute environment on a typed, versioned field.* — the author's phrase kept, grounded. (Runners-up: *knowledge that computes* · *one medium for knowledge, compute and interface* · *substrate to surface*.)

- **One sentence.** *A compute environment where knowledge, programs, models and the interface itself share one typed, versioned field — so everything anyone or anything writes is structure the next can stand on.*

- **The mechanism sentence.** *Completion from a place: a model's context is an address in the field, its output typed structure back into it — and the interface composes from the same contracts.*

- **The paragraph** is the README's head — the ladder's proof is that each rung compresses the rung below without lying.

## Pairings that hold the tension

The system lives in dualities; a good name will sit inside one of them.

- **substrate × surface** — the system's own two ends; the whole architecture in three words: *one field, substrate to surface*.
- **typed × lived** — a type system you inhabit; contracts as the ground of an environment, not a checker beside it.
- **knowledge × compute** — data that runs, programs that know; the two estates one medium joins.
- **medium × environment** — not a tool you open; a medium you inhabit (the README's own close).
- **place × completion** — the claim as a pairing; where the model meets the field.
- **field × citizen** — everything (person, program, model, component) as a citizen of one field; nothing bolted on.
- **commit × compound** — every act recorded, every record built upon.

## What any framing must survive

- **Honesty**: no rung may claim what the pilot does not architecturally hold.
- **Both estates**: a framing that says only knowledge (a notes tool) or only compute (a runtime) fails; the join *is* the thing.
- **The interface counted in**: most framings forget that what draws is field data too — the rarest part of the claim, keep it in the sentence rung.
- **Room to grow**: knowledge is the first tenant, not the last (sound, image, each other — README); a framing bound to "documents" or "AI" ages badly.

## Name directions — seeds only, none proposed

A name should be one rung of the ladder survived to a word. By pairing:

- *field/place*: Field, Openfield, Locus — plain, spatial, matches "completion from a place".
- *ground/medium*: Loam, Ground, Bedrock — the compounding-substrate feel; loam is alive where bedrock is inert.
- *joining*: Loom, Ply, Splice — composition-forward. (**Weft is taken** — a documented peer project, [`weft.md`](weft.md).)
- *growth/compounding*: Coppice, Mycelium-family — organic compounding; risk of trend-scent.
- *light* (the incumbent): OpenLight — openness and clarity; says nothing of field, type, or compute; carries the project's history.

Test for any candidate: put it in the sentence rung — "*X* is a compute environment where …" — and see whether the name pulls its weight or rides along.
