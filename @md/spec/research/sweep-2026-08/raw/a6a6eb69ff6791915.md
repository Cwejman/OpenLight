# 2025–2026 status: open-source & dev-oriented low-code / no-code / internal-tool platforms

All GitHub figures pulled live from `https://api.github.com/repos/{owner}/{repo}` and `/releases` on **2026-08-26**.

## Open-source internal-tool builders

**Appsmith** — `appsmithorg/appsmith`: 40,748 stars, latest release **v2.3, 2026-08-13**, pushed 2026-08-26, Apache-2.0. Still genuinely maintained. History: $41M Series B ([TechCrunch, 2022-06-22](https://techcrunch.com/2022/06/22/appsmith-takes-open-source-route-to-accelerate/)), then **25% layoff** ([Inc42, 2023-10-26](https://inc42.com/buzz/exclusive-insight-partners-backed-appsmith-lays-off-25-of/)). AI pivot: **Appsmith Agents** announced [Business Wire, 2025-05-01](https://www.businesswire.com/) and [Show HN, 2025-04-29](https://agents.appsmith.com/blog/agent-templates-now-available-in-appsmith-agents). No acquisition found. Notable 2026 event is a security one: CVE-2026-22794 account-takeover, actively exploited ([SC Media, 2026-01-26](https://www.scmagazine.com/)). No funding or revenue disclosure since 2022 — the $10.1M ARR figure circulating is GetLatka, **UNVERIFIED**.
**Grade: shipped-and-adopted** (flat commercially, AI features shipped).

**Budibase** — `Budibase/budibase`: 28,242 stars, **v3.43.0, 2026-08-24**, very high release cadence. Repo description now reads "**AI agents, automations and apps that run your operations. Model agnostic.**" — an explicit repositioning. **Budibase Agents Beta** [2026-03-19](https://budibase.com/blog/updates/ai-agents-beta/). Last funding: $7M seed, [TechCrunch 2022-11-04](https://techcrunch.com/2022/11/04/meet-budibase-a-low-code-open-source-web-app-builder-with-automations/). Unauthenticated RCE disclosed by Endor Labs, 2026-04-07.
**Grade: shipped-and-adopted**, pivoting to AI.

**ToolJet** — `ToolJet/ToolJet`: 40,766 stars, **v3.20.216-lts, 2026-08-25**, AGPL-3.0, pushed daily. Hardest AI pivot of the group: repo description is now "the open-source foundation of **ToolJet AI** — the enterprise app generation platform." Separate product site tooljet.ai launched Feb 2025 ([Show HN, 2025-02-12](https://www.tooljet.ai/); [EIN Presswire, 2025-02-11](https://www.einpresswire.com/)). Funding on record: $1.5M seed (2022), $4.6M pre-Series A ([SiliconANGLE, 2023-02-02](https://siliconangle.com/2023/02/02/tooljet-reels-4-6m-low-code-application-development-platform/)), plus Microsoft M12 + GitHub Fund ([Business Wire, 2023-07-17](https://www.businesswire.com/)). **The "$8.5M ARR" claim is GetLatka (2026-08-10) — UNVERIFIED, no company or press confirmation found.** tooljet.ai claims "1,000+ companies" (self-reported).
**Grade: shipped-and-adopted**, AI-first repositioning.

**Directus** — `directus/directus`: 37,468 stars, **v12.3.1, 2026-08-25**. This is the big story. Sequence:
- GPLv3 → **BSL 1.1**, 2023-04 ([directus.io/bsl](https://directus.io/bsl/); [HN, 2023-04-27](https://news.ycombinator.com/) "Directus is no longer open source").
- Native **MCP** support, v11.13, [2025-11-07](https://directus.com/resources); covered by [CMS Critic, 2025-11-05](https://cmscritic.com/).
- v11.15 (2026-02-12): AI Assistant GA + collaborative editing; v11.16 (2026-03-10): multimodal AI.
- **"Evolving Our License for Long-Term Sustainability," 2026-04-22**, then **Directus 12 (2026-06-10)**: relicensed **BUSL-1.1 → MSCL-1.0-GPL** (Monospace Sustainable Core License) *and* introduced **active license-key enforcement**. Self-hosted defaults to a capped "Core" tier — **3 seats, 25 collections, 5 Flows**; SSO, custom permission rules, custom/self-hosted LLMs and AI translations **stop working** without a key (30-day grace on upgrade). Sources: [release notes v12.0.0](https://github.com/directus/directus/releases/tag/v12.0.0), [directus.com/docs/licensing/overview](https://directus.com/docs/licensing/overview), [directus.com/pricing](https://directus.com/pricing). Free commercial use only via an "Open Innovation Grant" for entities **<$5M revenue and <50 employees**. HN noticed: "Self-hosted Directus 12 requires a license key to lift caps" ([2026-06-17](https://news.ycombinator.com/)).
- Domain moved directus.io → **directus.com**. Team plan is **$499–599/mo**, extra seats $50/mo.
- **No acquisition, no funding round, no layoffs found.** Last disclosed raise remains $7M Series A ([PR Newswire, 2022-11-10](https://www.prnewswire.com/)). The license tightening reads as a monetization squeeze in lieu of new capital.
**Grade: shipped-and-adopted** as software; **source-available, not open source** since 2023 and now gated by default.

**NocoDB** — `nocodb/nocodb`: **64,733 stars** (largest here), release **2026.08.1, 2026-08-19**, pushed 2026-08-26. Last funding $10.5M, [TechCrunch 2022-10-11](https://techcrunch.com/2022/10/11/nocodb-airtable-open-source/). No 2025–26 press coverage, funding, or license news found. **Grade: shipped-and-adopted** (code very alive; company quiet).

**Baserow** — has **moved primary hosting off GitLab to `github.com/baserow/baserow`** (5,706 stars, release **2.3.3, 2026-07-21**, pushed 2026-08-25). **Baserow 2.0** shipped [2025-11-19](https://baserow.io/blog/baserow-2-0-release-notes) — self-hosted data platform with automations and AI; a January 2026 HN post frames it as "databases, automations and **agents** with AI." **Grade: shipped-niche**, growing, AI features added.

## No-code / commercial

**Bubble** — active and fully AI-pivoted. **Bubble AI Agent** launched [Business Wire, 2025-10-16](https://www.businesswire.com/) ("first AI agent for visual development"); [2026-08-04](https://bubble.io/blog) it gained native mobile app generation and auto-fixing. Last funding is still the $100M Series A ([SiliconANGLE, 2021-07-27](https://siliconangle.com/2021/07/27/bubble-announces-100m-funding-no-code-solution/)); **no CEO change, layoffs, or new round found**. Downside signal: Bubble-built apps used at scale for Microsoft credential phishing ([BleepingComputer, 2026-03-25](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/); [Kaspersky, 2026-03-24](https://www.kaspersky.com/)). **Grade: shipped-and-adopted.**

**Webflow** — repositioning + contraction. Acquired **Intellimize** (2024) and **Vidoso** (AI content generation) [TechCrunch, 2026-03-12](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/12/webflow-buys-ai-content-generation-platform-vidoso-to-bolster-its-marketing-suite/); >$330M raised; CEO **Linda Tong**: "people have historically seen us as a website builder or CMS. We are an **agentic marketing platform**." Then **abrupt layoffs, 2026-05-27/28** — [SF Chronicle](https://www.sfchronicle.com/tech/article/webflow-layoffs-tech-san-francisco-22279561.php) ("it was a bloodbath"), widely covered; staff reported laptop lockout before notification. **I could not verify the headcount or percentage cut** — do not quote a number. **No ARR disclosure and no IPO filing found.** **Grade: shipped-and-adopted**, but contracting.

**Glide** — rebranded to **GlideOS**, open beta **August 2026**; [MCP client support](https://www.glideapps.com/blog) shipped Aug 2026; internally rebuilt engineering around AI agents ("Slipstream"). Last funding: $20M Series A ([TechCrunch, 2022-04-21](https://techcrunch.com/2022/04/21/after-proving-need-for-no-code-apps-glide-rewarded-with-20m-series-a/)). GetLatka's "$3.7M ARR" — **UNVERIFIED**. **Grade: shipped-niche**, pivoting to AI.

**Softr** — relaunched as an **AI-native no-code platform with "AI Co-Builder," 2026-03-31** ([SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/03/31/softr-launches-ai-native-no-code-business-software-building-platform/), [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/)). No funding, ARR, or headcount disclosed. **Grade: shipped-niche**, AI pivot.

**Adalo** — alive but small. **AnyData API platform for on-premise enterprise apps**, [GlobeNewswire, 2026-03-06](https://www.globenewswire.com/); "Ada" AI app generation covered [AI CERTs, 2026-03-10](https://aicerts.ai/). Last funding: $8M Series A, 2021. **Grade: shipped-niche.**

**Zapier** — [Enterprise AI governance across "every surface where building happens," Business Wire, 2026-04-23](https://www.businesswire.com/); published **AutomationBench**, with an independent leaderboard appearing 2026-07-06. Warning sign: [PPC Land, 2026-08-24](https://ppc.land/) — "Agents burn 5x more tokens than humans as **Zapier traffic drops**." The "$310M ARR / $5B valuation" figure is **GetLatka — UNVERIFIED**; the $5B valuation itself traces to 2021 secondary reporting. **Grade: shipped-and-adopted.**

**Make.com** — owned by **Celonis** (acquired Integromat, 2020). Celonis remains acquisitive (Ikigai Labs, 2026-05-13). **No revenue, ARR, customer, or divestiture disclosure for Make found in 2025–26 reporting.** **Grade: shipped-and-adopted / financials unknown.**
