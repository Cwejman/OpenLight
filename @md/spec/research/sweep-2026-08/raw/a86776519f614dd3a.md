## DITA 2.0: still not released (as of 2026-08-26)

**Grade: announced-only, 6 years past its promised date.** The OASIS DITA 2.0 milestone stands at **85% complete, 9 open / 52 closed issues, no due date**, last touched 2026-05-08 (https://github.com/oasis-tcs/dita/milestone/2). The TC's own minutes of **28 April 2026** are the most damning primary source: Robert Anderson (Oracle) reported the spec build now works under 2.0; outstanding work is element/attribute docs, CN transforms, a migration guide (Steinbacher) and a troubleshooting guide (Eberlein/Harrison/Stevens). Keith Schengili-Roberts noted the last 1.3 update was **2018** and 2.0 was **promised for 2020**; the TC characterised this as "a communications issue, not a progress issue" and assigned Eberlein to draft a status note to dita-users. **No AI discussion appears in the minutes** (https://groups.oasis-open.org/discussion/dita-tc-minutes-28-april-2026-uploaded). No Committee Specification Draft public review for 2.0 surfaced in 2025–2026.

**Tooling: preview-only.** DITA-OT **4.4 (2026-01-31)** ships "a preview of features for the upcoming OASIS DITA 2.0 specification… on the basis of the latest drafts of the DITA 2.0 DTD and RELAX NG grammar files from OASIS (as of January 25, 2026)", with the explicit caveat "Other new or revised features proposed for DITA 2.0 are not yet supported" (https://www.dita-ot.org/dev/reference/dita-v2-0-support.html). Implemented so far: `<keytext>`, `<linktitle>`, revised `<navtitle>` class, new chunking module, DITAVAL `@add-outputclass`. **4.4.1 (2026-08-21)** is bug fixes only (https://www.dita-ot.org/dev/release-notes/). Oxygen **28.0 (2025-11-26)** and **28.1 (2026-03-18)** list *no* DITA 2.0 features — the DITA work is UX incrementalism (DITA Maps Manager redesign; change-history form controls) (https://www.oxygenxml.com/xml_editor/whats_new.html). Adobe RoboHelp's **March 2026** release is publishing/conditional-expression fixes; FrameMaker retains DITA authoring, nothing 2.0 (https://helpx.adobe.com/robohelp/using/whats-new-robohelp-2026.html). **Nobody is shipping DITA 2.0.** Grade: paper-only spec, shipped-niche preview tooling.

## Structural shock: STC is gone

**Grade: shipped-and-confirmed fact, widely under-weighted.** The Society for Technical Communication **ceased operations 29 January 2025** and filed **Chapter 7** (case **1:25-bk-11577**, E.D. Va.) — membership had fallen from ~25,000 at its early-2000s peak to **~4,500** (https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Society_for_Technical_Communication; https://www.bkalerts.com/recent-bankruptcy-cases/virginia-eastern-bankruptcy-court/1:25-bk-11577/). There is **no STC Summit** in 2025 or 2026; any live summit.stc.org page is stale. Anyone citing "STC's current position statement" is citing a defunct organisation. The surviving venues are CIDM (ConVEx Pittsburgh **13–15 Apr 2026**; ConVEx IDEAS online **22–24 Sep 2026**, https://infomanagementcenter.com/cidm-conferences/), DITA Europe + DITA-OT Day (10th edition, **1 Feb 2026, Brussels**, https://www.oxygenxml.com/events/2026/dita-ot_day.html), and tekom.

## tekom's actual position

**Grade: opinion/organisational, not survey.** The "Report 2025" is an **activity report, not research** — no sample, no adoption data; its only numbers are 27 election candidates and session counts (https://www.technical-communication.org/report-2025). **I found no current tekom/tcworld industry survey with structured-authoring adoption figures.** tekom's published position papers are regulatory, not architectural: the **AI Act** paper (2024-05-10, https://www.technical-communication.org/fileadmin/tekom.eu/Technical_Communication/2024_05_10_tekom_Europe_Position_Paper_AI_Act.pdf) and a Machinery Regulation 2023/1230 paper (Jan 2026). A tekom Europe **manifesto is still in draft**, continuing at the spring 2026 assembly. tcworld conference 2026 (**10–12 Nov, Stuttgart**) lists nine subject areas — **DITA and iiRDS are not among them**; AI and "Intelligent Delivery" are (https://tcworldconference.tekom.de/program).

## iiRDS

**Grade: shipped-niche, still being pump-primed 9 years in.** **v1.3 released 2025-11-03**: new **iiRDS/H** handover-documentation profile integrating **VDI 2770**, plus optional **JSON-LD** serialisation (https://www.iirds.org/materials/version-13). Consortium: **27 members** (https://iirds.org/iirds-consortium/members/) — overwhelmingly German tech-comm service providers and tool vendors (Docufy, Empolis, Noxum, Quanos, parson, plusmeta, kothes, Pantopix) plus five industrial adopters (Siemens, SEW-Eurodrive, Endress+Hauser, GEA, Heller). Three small US members joined 2025–26 (Intuitive Stack, Clio's Techscribe, EnduraDocs). The tell on adoption: the consortium still **funds pilots at up to €5,000 net per project**, applications open to **2026-09-30**, grant period to 2027-04-30, with mandatory conference presentation (https://www.iirds.org/tools/pilot-projects). A standard subsidising €5k pilots in year nine is not one the market is pulling. The AI turn gave it a *narrative* (a non-normative guide covering LLM auto-annotation), not measurable uptake.

## "Structured content is better for AI": no evidence found

**Grade: FOLKLORE / opinion.** I could locate **no study comparing a DITA/XML corpus against an unstructured corpus** for RAG quality. A widely-echoed "**structured knowledge bases outperform unstructured by 29–46 percentage points**" claim surfaced in search summaries attributed to Heretto — fetching the cited page shows **it contains no such figure**; the only statistic there is "80% of business information begins as unstructured," sourced to a **ScienceDirect topic index page, not a study** (https://www.heretto.com/blog/structured-vs-unstructured-content). Treat both as folklore. The nearest real work is PDF-based and not DITA: arXiv **2506.23136** (structure-aware RAG on three electrical-equipment manuals, 50 questions; faithfulness 0.866 vs 0.556 baseline) — small-n, no XML. Meanwhile the RAG literature partly *cuts against* the claim: a NAACL 2025 Findings result reports fixed 200-word chunks matching or beating semantic chunking on retrieval and generation. What evidence exists supports **heading hierarchy and metadata enrichment**, which Markdown provides as well as DITA does.

## CCMS market

**Grade: opinion dressed as data.** 2025 market-size estimates range **$2.14B to $4.6B** across report mills (Verified Market Research, Reports and Data, Global Insight Services, Cognitive Market Research) — a >2x spread means none are usable. **There is no Gartner MQ for CCMS.** Capital activity is quiet: MadCap has been **Battery Ventures**-owned since 2022-01-11 (IXIASOFT 2022, Xyleme Jan 2024); Heretto's last round was **$17M, 2022-12-05** ($21.7M total, PitchBook); Paligo's was **$29M, 2023-04-17**. **No 2025–26 funding or consolidation events found.**

## Read

DITA is **stable-but-stalled, not revived**: the spec is six years late, tooling is preview-only, the professional body that carried it in North America is bankrupt, and tekom's conference taxonomy no longer names it. The revival is **rhetorical, and it is happening at the conferences, not in the standard**. DITA Europe 2026 Day 2 is ~50% AI (Adobe "Agentic Support on a Structured Foundation"; Fluid Topics "Agentic AI"/MCP; Cisco "**Why DITA in 2026: The AI Age**"; Gollner "DITA and Enterprise AI"), against **one** session on the spec itself — Anderson's "Upgrading the DITA Specification" (https://ditaeurope.infomanagementcenter.com/2026-agenda-day-2/). BLS projects **1% growth** for technical writers 2024–34 and explicitly names genAI as a demand limiter (https://www.bls.gov/ooh/media-and-communication/technical-writers.htm).
