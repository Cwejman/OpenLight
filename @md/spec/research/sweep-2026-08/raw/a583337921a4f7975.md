## 2. THE LADDER

Still **fifteen**, unchanged in `knowledge-structure.md`. (What changed is that a rival now exists: `code.md` proposes to replace all fifteen with two values, eight facts and six principles.) One line each, as written:

1. Every head is bounded — person's comprehension and agent's context window are "one limitation."
2. Knowledge nests — a *fold*: readable alone, entered from above, descended by reference.
3. What is met first weighs most — purpose first, gates last. *(carries an explicit Open: is a reading order one line or a tree?)*
4. One fact, one home — single source of truth for knowledge.
5. Untracked change is drift — knowledge as code.
6. A blank session is the same for everyone — no agent memory, no private context.
7. Home follows lifetime — transcripts/log/drafts feed hardened state, "the **spec**" *(open vocabulary; lean)*.
8. A claim carries its ground and its status — unmarked reads settled.
9. Questions have an address — disagreement lands on a paragraph.
10. Nothing is lost silently — *lossless*.
11. Written whole, around what it establishes — folded in by rewriting, never patched.
12. The personal is knowledge, and its speaker owns it.
13. Don't run ahead of comprehension.
14. Knowledge compounds — *(explicitly Open: "this rung has the most left to study and ground")*.
15. Beauty is comprehension.

## 5. OPEN QUESTIONS A WEB SWEEP COULD PLAUSIBLY ANSWER

Tractable by literature:

- **Does the redundancy effect apply to agents?** Chart: "do people and agents want incompatible wikis?" The code makes this **first among its opens** — the one-artifact claim vs context-rot evidence, and whether folds need a derived claim-list twin for machine readers.
- **Does an agent chunk at all**, or are fold headers its only chunking mechanism rather than a convenience?
- **Is there a size ceiling on "written whole"** — does rewrite-whole survive a corpus past what one rewrite holds?
- **The Diátaxis challenge:** must a fold blending explanation, how-to and reference split into typed files behind one entry? The code calls this "the strongest outside challenge to the fold."
- **Which wiki facts are derivable from the repo** — the untouched OpenAPI/Javadoc/JSDoc territory is exactly the evidence base for the tangle-don't-retype rule.
- **Does compounding hold for an agent reader** — is absorptive capacity bounded by prior shared context, or does retrieval flatten it? Related: can rung 14 be stated with no cost-ratio implication and still bite?
- **What is the test for "this cannot be written"** — the codify-vs-personalize boundary (Hansen 80/20 is the one lead).
- **Depth/breadth numbers** — Miller 1981, Kiger 1984, Larson & Czerwinski are cited but unfollowed; the code explicitly holds "two levels, three at most" as "leads from the chart, not law."
- **Onboarding:** the "human reader is not blank" finding — mentors and response latency dominating documentation — is named open by the code and concrete for hjulverkstan (interns and a client).
- Also verifiable rather than answerable: Cowan 2001, Sweller 2019's element-interactivity gate, Chase & Simon, Romer 1990, Ausubel's effect size, Mar et al.'s *g*, RULER and *Lost in the Middle*, and the Eberhardt/Böckeler SDD cost figures.

Not answerable by a sweep, and named as such: whether the code supersedes the ladder; who works the queue; what the fold's grain becomes in the substrate; and the inward map of hjulverkstan's own outcomes and audiences, which `study.md` says "has not started."
